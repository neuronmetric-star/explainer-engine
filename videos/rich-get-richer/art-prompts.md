# Art prompts: rich-get-richer (Money Heck)

**How:** new ChatGPT chat → paste **START** → **BATCH 1** (4 backgrounds) → send Claude the images → Claude checks them →
**BATCH 2** (6 props on green). Reused (nothing to paint): the dollar bill, cash pile and phone from older videos, the E01 sunset ending, the helicopter.

**Color script:** B01 coffee shop (warm morning amber: a normal day) → B02 roadside rain (cold blue-gray, orange hazard lights: stuck) →
B03 money tower cutaway (steel street level → green bank → gold penthouse: money rolls uphill) → B04 penthouse (golden hour: calm, rich) → the sunset goodbye.

```
START
You are the lead background painter for a premium vertical animated series. Every image is a finished, cinematic background painting for a high-end 2D animated film, made for a 9:16 phone screen. Follow this style bible exactly:

STYLE: rich 2D storybook painting with feature-film polish. Appealing, slightly exaggerated cartoon shapes (chunky furniture, oversized props, soft rounded silhouettes), but painted with real depth and detail: layered painterly brushwork, soft gradients, subtle paper grain, crisp edges on the hero object, softer edges in the distance. Every place looks lived-in and specific, never empty or generic: dense, organized set dressing, small storytelling details, believable clutter at the edges.
DEPTH: always three layers plus air: a foreground element cropped by the frame edge, the stage and hero in the middle, a deep background, then atmosphere (haze, dust in light shafts, steam, rain, glow). Distant things get lighter, bluer and softer.
LIGHT: cinematic and motivated. A clear key light from the direction I give, plus practical light sources inside the scene (lamps, neon, screens, windows, headlights) with colored glow, warm-cool contrast, colored shadows (blue or purple, never gray or black), rim light on edges, reflections on glossy and wet floors.
COLOR: a harmonious palette with one strong accent color, as I specify per image.
TEXTURE: every surface has a material: wood grain, scuffed tile, brushed metal, marble, wet asphalt, fabric, glass reflections, brass.
COMPOSITION: portrait 2:3. Leave the open floor zone I describe clear and flat for characters to stand (texture and reflections are fine, objects are not). Keep the band at 60-72% of the height, in the center, calm and low-contrast (captions go there). Keep the top 7% and bottom 20% simple.
STRICT: no people, no characters, no animals, no faces, no hands, no feet, no silhouettes of people. No numbers, prices or dollar signs anywhere. No money bills painted in the backgrounds (I add the money later). No text, letters or logos unless I give the exact words in quotes; all other signs, screens and labels stay blank or show simple pictogram icons.

HOW TO WORK: I'll send prompts in numbered batches. Make every image in the batch one after another, in order, portrait 2:3. Above each image write only its file name. Props go on a solid pure green #00FF00 background with no shadow on the background. When a batch is done, say "BATCH DONE".
```
