# Sources: why do the poor get poorer and the rich get richer

Mode: **Explainer, track one object** (one dollar) · Money Heck ep. 1 (Brian, Nov 25 2026). Search phrase: **why do the poor get poorer and the rich get richer**.
Payoff (one sentence): when you're short on money you pay fees and interest; that money flows to banks, banks pay their owners, and the owners
are mostly the rich, so the same dollar rolls uphill; the rich own the thing that collects, the poor pay it.
Not advice: we explain how the money moves; no "you should" lines. Page fetches were blocked in this session; facts confirmed across search results.

## Labels
- Doug (short on cash) and Victor (rich) are invented characters; the $3 balance, $5 coffee, car repair are an illustration.
- "Doug's fee became Victor's payday" is a **simplified** picture of the real chain: fees and interest are bank income → bank profit →
  dividends and a higher stock price for the bank's owners (shareholders) → most stock is owned by the richest 10% (fact 8). Say it as a chain, never "Victor took it".
