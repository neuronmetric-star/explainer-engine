# ---- CHAPTER 3: Free Toys ----
[#ch3] [#trick] So how do you make money from something free? [#toy] Imagine a toy store that gives away every toy for free. [#watch] But it watches what you play with. [#sells] Then it charges other companies to put the exact toy you want right in front of you. That's an ad.
[#product] You're not the customer. You're the !product.
[#ipo] In {2012,|twenty twelve,} Facebook goes public, valued at about {*$104 billion.|a hundred and four billion dollars.}
[#lunch] And when a new kid got popular at the lunch table? [#insta] In this case, Instagram. A photo app with {13|thirteen} employees, and about {30 million|thirty million} users.
