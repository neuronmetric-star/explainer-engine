# Sources: Why do Lamborghini supercars exist? (Ferruccio Lamborghini vs Enzo Ferrari)

**Real people, partly disputed story.** Ferruccio Lamborghini and Enzo Ferrari are real. On screen they are played by our
silent cast (polo guy = Ferruccio, Victor = Enzo), a **reconstruction**. The clutch-and-insult story is the famous origin
legend. It comes from **Ferruccio's own retelling** (1991 interview) and from Lamborghini test driver **Valentino Balboni**,
who heard it from him. Earlier test driver Bob Wallace doubted it. The script attributes both key claims ("His test
driver later said…", "According to Ferruccio…") instead of stating them as fact. Wikipedia pages could not be fetched
directly from this session (network policy); facts were confirmed through search results and the outlets below.

## Checked and left out (for length)
- First car: the 350 GTV/350 GT had a V12 engine designed by Giotto Bizzarrini (a former Ferrari engineer).
- The gold raging-bull badge became official in 1972 (Motor1). Ferruccio also loved bullfighting (secondary sources).
