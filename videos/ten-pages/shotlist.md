**Art:** 6 backgrounds + 8 props = **14 images**, all in ChatGPT from `art-prompts.md` (3 batches). Save them under the names
given into `videos/ten-pages/art/`.
**OpenArt video inserts:** none needed. Everything is people, books and small props, and the engine handles all of it.
**Locked objects (same in every image):** **the book** is a hardcover with a deep teal cover, a small gold star on the front and cream pages.
**The bookmark** is a red ribbon bookmark with a gold tassel. **The phone** is a black smartphone on a white charging cable. **The room** has the bed on the left,
the reading armchair and floor lamp on the right, and the dresser with the phone charger on the far wall.
**The plant:** the red bookmark is already sticking out of the teal book on the nightstand in the first shot, before it's mentioned at `bookmark`.
