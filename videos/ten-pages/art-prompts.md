# ChatGPT batch file: ten-pages (14 images)

**How to use:** open a **new ChatGPT chat**. Paste everything from **START** down to the **BATCH 1** line
and send. Then paste BATCH 1. When ChatGPT says "BATCH DONE", paste BATCH 2, and so on. Save each image
under the file name written above it into `videos/ten-pages/art/` (or send them here and I'll save them).
If the teal book or the bedroom layout drifts, attach `01_bedroom_wide_night.png` or `P01_book_closed.png` to the prompt as a reference.

```
START
You are the background artist for a vertical animated explainer series. For every image I ask for, follow this style bible exactly:

STYLE: flat 2D storybook illustration for a modern animated show. Clean, simple shapes with soft painterly gradients, subtle paper grain, bold readable silhouettes, slightly exaggerated cartoon proportions (chunky furniture, oversized props). No outlines on the background, or very thin darker outlines only.
COLOR & LIGHT: rich, saturated but harmonious colors. Always a clear light direction: warm light on lit sides, cool blue or purple in shadows. Gentle atmospheric haze. Dark scenes are deep blue, never pure black.
COMPOSITION: portrait 2:3. Leave the lower-middle of the frame as an open, flat floor where characters can stand. Keep the top 10% simple (ceiling, wall). Strong depth: something in the foreground, the main subject in the middle, a background further away.
STRICT: no people, no characters, no animals, no faces, no hands. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere (book covers and spines are blank except for the shapes I describe).

LOCKED OBJECTS (draw them exactly the same in every image they appear in, never redesign them):
- THE BOOK: a hardcover book with a deep teal cover, a small gold five-pointed star in the middle of the front cover, cream-colored pages, slightly rounded corners.
- THE BOOKMARK: a red satin ribbon bookmark with a small gold tassel at its end.
- THE PHONE: a plain black smartphone with a white charging cable.
- THE BEDROOM: a cozy small bedroom. Bed with a mustard-yellow blanket on the LEFT, a small wooden nightstand next to it; a soft green armchair with a warm brass floor lamp and a little round side table on the RIGHT; a wooden dresser against the FAR WALL in the middle, with the phone charger on top; a window on the far wall to the right of the dresser; a round blue rug on a wooden floor.

HOW TO WORK: I'll send prompts in numbered batches. Make every image in the batch one after another, in order, portrait 2:3. Above each image write only its file name. Props go on a solid pure green #00FF00 background with no shadow on the background. When a batch is done, say "BATCH DONE".
```
