# Sources: yawn-why ("Why do we yawn?") · Body Heck episode 2

Researched Nov 25 2026 (web search). Search phrase: "why do we yawn". Scout skill: **not run** (no new creator study; no trend sheet today, scrape tool banned).

## Fiction / reconstruction (labeled)
- Doug (can't stop yawning), Gary (sleeps through it) and every scene with them: **invented characters**; the "fixes" Doug tries are a teaching device.
- Doug "ran laps" and "breathed pure oxygen" are the story version of what researchers tested on volunteers.

## Careful wording (the science is NOT settled)
- Brain cooling is "the leading idea", not a proven answer: the script says "the best idea so far" and ends on "nobody's sure".
- The rat result (brain temperature rose before a yawn and fell after) is left out of the script to keep it short and plain.
- Contagious yawning: the empathy link is mixed (some studies yes, a 2014 Duke-led study found no strong link), so the script says only that nobody knows why.
- No yawn duration and no "% of people who catch yawns" is stated: **not reported** in the sources found.
- No medical advice: we explain how it works.
