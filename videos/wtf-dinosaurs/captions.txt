Why the fuck did one rock kill the dinosaurs? It wasn't the hit. It was the darkness. Here's how it works. 66 million years ago, Tina the Triceratops is eating ferns. Then a rock about 10 km wide slams into Mexico at up to 72,000 km/h. It leaves a crater about 180 km wide. Step one: fire. Debris rains back down, the sky heats up, and forests burn. Step two: darkness. Soot, dust and sulfur block the sun. Plants get under 1% of normal light for over a year. Step three: starvation. No sun, no plants. No plants, no Tina. No Tina, no T. rex. About 75% of all species, gone. But there's one twist. Not every dinosaur died. Birds made it. Scientists think beaks let them eat seeds. Meanwhile, tiny mammals hid in burrows. Those were our ancestors. The asteroid didn't kill the dinosaurs. It turned off the sun. And that pigeon outside your window? Still a dinosaur. Follow for more why the fuck science.
