and send. Then paste BATCH 1. When ChatGPT says "BATCH DONE", paste BATCH 2, and so on. Save each image
with the file name written above it into `videos/who-is-trump/art/`.
No people are painted: the president character is added by the 3D engine. Exact words (TRUMP, plaques, stamps) are added by the engine too, except P01.
