# Shot list: "Who the heck is Donald Trump?" (short, ~1:12)

**Cast (3D, silent, mouths still):** **The President** = new `president` outfit (`CHAR.make({outfit:'president'})`):
stocky, navy suit, white shirt, long red tie, flag pin, swept golden hair, white sphere head like the rest of the cast.
Neutral portrayal: he acts with gestures (`point`, `fired`, `thumbsup`, `present`, `tada`, `hips`, `cross`), never mocked.
He is the only cast member. His dad, contestants, voters are never shown as cast (buildings, props, crowd painting only).
**Art:** 7 backgrounds + 6 props = **13 images**, all in ChatGPT from `art-prompts.md` (3 batches) → `videos/who-is-trump/art/`.
**OpenArt video insert (optional, 1):** `07` election stage → crowd cheering + confetti for "He won." (crowds = PLAYBOOK §4.1).
Image-to-video from Brian's painting, no cast in the clip, one test clip first, credits reported.
**The plant:** the gold **TRUMP** letters (P01) on the tower at "Trump Tower opened" → they reappear stamped onto other
builders' towers at "The name still sold" → and on the final shot "The name never did."
**Engine text** (not painted, so spelling is exact): "THE TRUMP ORGANIZATION" plaque, "TRUMP" stamps on skyline towers,
"BANKRUPT" stamp, "THE APPRENTICE" title on the TV, year tags (1946, 1983, 2016, 2020, 2024), "45" / "47" badges, "Tell me below 💬" bubble.
