# Sources: Who the heck is Donald Trump?

**Doug, Gary, the tower they stand at and the lobby TV moment are fictional** (a framing device).
Every fact about Donald Trump is real. Tone is neutral: the narrator's jokes target Doug, never Trump.
Network note: millercenter.org / npr member sites were blocked in this session; facts below were confirmed via web search across multiple outlets.
