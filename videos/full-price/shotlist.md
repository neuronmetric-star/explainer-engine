# Shot list: "Why the heck do stores even need Black Friday deals?" (short, ≈ 1:08–1:11)

**Script:** approved Nov 22 2026 (`script.txt`, 211 words incl. CTA C; dollar-a-second's 208 words ran 1:11).
**Mode:** True story (JCPenney 2012), told through a Doug/Gary skit riding the "He wanna pay full price" trend.
**Search phrases:** "black friday deals" + "he wanna pay full price". **On-screen title for the first 2 s** (engine overlay,
top band): `BLACK FRIDAY DEALS` (white) / `HE WANNA PAY FULL PRICE` (gold). Both phrases in caption line 1.
**Cast** (nobody talks; mouths still; the narrator performs Gary's lines in his own voice, no accent):
- **Doug** = `hoodie`: the customer.
- **Gary** = `oxford` + glasses: the sales guy. Gestures on his lines, then **dances**.
- **Polo guy** = `{outfit:'polo', shirt:0x2f55b0}` (blue polo, a nod to the sketch): Gary's coworker. He joins the dance on the chant.
- **Victor** = `suit`: stand-in for "the boss" (JCPenney's CEO). He's a stand-in, not a likeness. He only does what the
  real CEO did: takes the sale signs down, then leaves with a box.

**The clue:** the yellow sale sticker on the TV box. It's on screen from `tv`, held in close-up at `plan`, and peeled at `sticker`.
**All prices and words are engine overlays.** The paintings and props have no text: blank sticker, blank tag,
blank payment card, blank signs. The engine writes `$599` (red, struck through), `$299` (gold), `4 EASY PAYMENTS`, `2012`, `−25%`,
`$4 BILLION` and `JCPenney` (plain caption-style type, not a logo).
**Gary's dance** (new pose loop, pure function of t): bounce `nod` ±.12 at 2 beats/s, `tilt` ±.15, `lean` ±.1 alternating,
`armR`/`armL` raise alternating .3↔.9 (hands waving), small side step. The polo guy runs the same loop half a beat late.
**Art (standard v2, PLAYBOOK §4):** 6 backgrounds + 8 props from `art-prompts.md` → `art/` (4 batches; batch 1 is reviewed first),
plus `H01_helicopter.png` copied from `docs/assets/`.
**Color script:** 01–02 electric dawn (blue + teal, warm register glow, red-gold accent) → 03 happy 2012 mall (honey gold,
sale-red) → 04 the store dies (gray-green fluorescent, one red cart) → 05 brain diagram (warm gold) → 06 morning after
(crisp blue sky, warm sun, teal balloons).
**Angles:** 01 low hero from the aisle · 02 close detail · 03/04 eye-level deep aisle · 05 straight-on diagram · 06 low wide from the lot.
**OpenArt:** none needed.
**Real chant clip:** not used. The narrator performs it. If Brian wants it later, it goes under `chant` (≈ 1.6 s).
