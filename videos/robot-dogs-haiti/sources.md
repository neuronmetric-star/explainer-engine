# Sources: Why there are robot dogs with rifles in Haiti

**Doug, Gary, the basement and the Roomba are fictional.** The operation, the footage and the robot-maker quote are real.
**Unverified (said on air as "it never fires"; flagged here):** no outlet has independently confirmed what the robots did in the operation,
who made them, or whether a human must pull the trigger. The "going first" job is what these robots are *built and sold for*, not a confirmed account of this raid.
