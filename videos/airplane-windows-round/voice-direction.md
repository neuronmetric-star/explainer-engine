# Voice direction: airplane-windows-round (Plane Heck ep. 2)

How to read the marks: **CAPS** = hit that word · `(slow)` = slow down, let it land · `(fast)` = quick and light ·
`…` = a short pause (half a breath) · `[SHOUT]` = full shout, like you just saw something crazy.
Dry and sarcastic, talking to one friend. After recording, Claude makes the shout louder and fine-tunes the speed
(`voice-drama.json`), so don't worry about perfect levels.

```
[SHOUT] WHAT THE HECK?!
… Why are airplane windows ROUND?
(slow) Ever seen a square window on a plane? … You haven't. … And the reason is kind of SCARY.
(fast) See, Doug had a problem. He's building one of the first jet planes ever, and he wants BIG square windows. Like a living room.
Honestly? … I'd want that window too.
(fast) Remember: planes pump themselves full of air, like a balloon. Every flight, the plane puffs up. Every landing, it shrinks back.
And every puff PULLS on the metal around the windows.
Try ripping a sheet of paper. … Hard, right? … Now make a TINY cut first. … It rips EASY.
(slow) A sharp corner works like that tiny cut. All the pull piles up … right there.
(slow, serious) And this really happened. In 1954, two of the first passenger jets, called Comets, broke apart in the sky.
Engineers sank a whole plane in a giant water tank and pumped it up and down, flight after flight.
(slow) After about three thousand flights … it CRACKED. … Right at a sharp square corner.
(warmer) So now every window is ROUND. No corners. The pull spreads out evenly, and there's nowhere for a crack to start.
(dry) Doug wanted a window like a living room. … He got one like a fishbowl.
Next flight, look for the corners. … There aren't any.
(fast, friendly) Still here? Then that means you enjoyed the video. So follow and like for more.
```

If your voice tool understands tags (ElevenLabs v3 does: `[shouts]`, `[pause]`, `[slowly]`), you can put them in place of the marks.
