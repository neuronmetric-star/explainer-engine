# Sources: airplane-windows-round ("Why are airplane windows round?") · Plane Heck episode 2

Researched Nov 24 2026 (web search). Search phrase: "why are airplane windows round".

## Fiction / reconstruction (labeled)
- Doug designing a square window and Gary in the seat: **invented characters** (series role: Doug builds airplanes).
- The paper-rip and "push spreads around a curve" comparisons are teaching analogies.

## Careful wording (myth vs fact)
- Popular articles say "square windows caused the Comet crashes". The inquiry evidence is more precise: the fatal cracks began at
  **sharp corners of cut-outs** in the pressurized fuselage (for G-ALYP, a rivet hole at the corner of a radio-antenna cut-out in the
  roof; in the water-tank test, at a corner of a squarish escape-hatch window). The script therefore says **"sharp square corners"**,
  never "the square passenger windows broke".
