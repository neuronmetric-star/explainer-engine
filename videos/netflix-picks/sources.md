# Sources: How Netflix chooses what to show you

**Doug, Gary, the bet and the rom-coms are fictional.** The Netflix facts are real.

| Claim in the script | Source |
|---|---|
| Netflix makes many artworks per title and picks the one each member is most likely to click, based on viewing history (contextual bandits) | Netflix TechBlog, "Artwork Personalization at Netflix" (Chandrashekar, Amat, Basilico, Jebara, Dec 2017), as covered by Dezeen: https://www.dezeen.com/2017/12/20/netflix-targets-film-artwork-depending-users-viewing-habits-design-technology/ · Reforge brief: https://www.reforge.com/blog/brief-netflix-artwork-personalization-through-multi-armed-bandit-testing |
| "Good Will Hunting: romance fans got the couple, comedy fans got Robin Williams": the TechBlog's own example (members who watch romance may see Matt Damon and Minnie Driver; comedy watchers may see Robin Williams) | same as above · It's Nice That: https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/netflix-personalises-artwork-to-viewing-habits-181217 |
| "you quit after about sixty to ninety seconds of scrolling": "a typical Netflix member loses interest after perhaps 60 to 90 seconds of choosing, having reviewed 10 to 20 titles" (consumer research cited in the paper) | Gomez-Uribe & Hunt, "The Netflix Recommender System", ACM TMIS 6(4), Dec 2015, as reported by NBC News: https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/netflix-knows-how-long-you-ll-search-they-lose-you-n521766 · Quartz: https://qz.com/622316/netflix-knows-exactly-how-long-youll-look-for-something-to-watch-before-giving-up |
| "About 80% of what people watch comes from those picks": the recommender "influences choice for about 80% of hours streamed" | same paper (2015), via RT Insights: https://www.rtinsights.com/netflix-recommendations-machine-learning-algorithms/ |
| "Netflix says it saves them over a billion dollars a year": "the combined effect of personalization and recommendations save us more than $1B per year" (Netflix's own estimate, from reduced cancellations) | same paper (2015), via Business Insider: https://www.businessinsider.in/why-netflix-thinks-its-personalized-recommendation-engine-is-worth-1-billion-per-year/articleshow/52754724.cms · Motley Fool: https://www.fool.com/investing/2016/06/19/how-netflixs-ai-saves-it-1-billion-every-year.aspx |

Note: the 60–90 s and 80% figures date from 2015. The video presents them as Netflix's research, not current measurements.
