# Sources — Why the fuck did China build the Great Wall?
- **Great Wall of China** — Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wall_of_China — 2012 survey: all dynasties' walls total 21,196 km (≈13,171 mi); Ming wall 8,851 km; built to defend against northern nomads; also taxed Silk Road goods and controlled migration; beacon towers for signals.
- **Great Wall of China** — HISTORY.com — https://www.history.com/articles/great-wall-of-china — Built over ~2,000 years by many dynasties; Qin Shi Huang linked earlier walls; most surviving wall is Ming.
- **Great Wall of China** — Britannica — https://britannica.com/topic/Great-Wall-of-China/Introduction — Defensive purpose vs nomadic peoples; Ming rebuilding from late 1400s.
- **The Great Wall of China Construction Project that Spanned Generations** — Ancient Origins — https://www.ancient-origins.net/node/11155 — Qin linked walls of Yan, Zhao and Qin from ~214 BC; in 1644 Wu Sangui opened Shanhai Pass to the Manchus.
- **Battle of Shanhai Pass** — Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Shanhai_Pass — Ming general Wu Sangui surrendered to the Qing (Manchus) at Shanhai Pass, May 1644.
- **Great Wall: Length, History, Why Built** — China Highlights — https://www.chinahighlights.com/greatwall/ — Beacon towers relayed smoke/fire signals; wall slowed mounted raiders.
