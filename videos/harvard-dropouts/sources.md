# Sources: Why Gates and Zuckerberg dropped out of Harvard (vs. most founders)

**Current script = Brian's v3 (`script.txt`). No fictional characters.** Everything is about the real Bill Gates and Mark Zuckerberg plus founder statistics.
On screen they are played by our silent look-alike cast (`gates`, `zuck`); they never speak. Jokes ("a toaster with ambition") are the narrator's opinion.
Older drafts (`script-v1-doug.txt`, `script-v2-claude.txt`) are not used.
**Fact fixes made to Brian's v3:** "More than a year later, Zuckerberg leaves Harvard" → "That summer" (he left summer 2004, about 4–6 months after the February launch); "Zuckerberg got his twelve" → "thirteen" (2004 → 2017).

**Notes**
- The two statistics come from different studies (Census/NBER for age; Kauffman for degrees), so the script states them as two separate sentences.
- Gates's exact leave date varies between accounts (1975, with a brief return in 1976); every account puts the Altair BASIC demo and MITS deal **before** he left for good, which is the only order the script relies on.
- Network note: the Kellogg PDF and computinghistory.org.uk pages could not be fetched from this session; their facts were checked against several search-result summaries and the NBER/Census versions of the same paper.
