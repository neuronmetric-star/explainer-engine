# Sources: $30 million now, or $1 every second for a year?

**Doug, Gary and the billionaire's offer are fictional** (a thought experiment, like the viral version below).
All numbers are real arithmetic or sourced. No financial advice: the video explains the math, it doesn't tell anyone what to pick.

| Claim in the script | Source / working |
|---|---|
| One day has 86,400 seconds; $1/s = $86,400 after day one | 60 × 60 × 24 = 86,400 (arithmetic) |
| Day twelve: Gary passes $1 million (a million seconds ≈ 11.6 days) | 1,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 11.57 days (arithmetic) |
| A year has 31,536,000 seconds → $31.5M, $1.5M more than $30M | 60 × 60 × 24 × 365 = 31,536,000 (non-leap year; arithmetic) |
| Good savings accounts pay about 4% a year right now | Fortune, "Best savings account rates, Nov 16 2026" (top 4.70% APY; many above 4.00%): https://fortune.com/article/best-savings-account-rates-11-16-2026/ · Bankrate, best high-yield savings (top listed 4.35%, Nov 2026): https://www.bankrate.com/banking/savings/best-high-yield-interests-savings-accounts/ · NerdWallet, Nov 2026: https://www.nerdwallet.com/best/banking/savings-accounts |
