# Shot list: "$30 million now, or $1 every second for a year?" (short, ≈ 1:05–1:10)

**Script:** locked (`script.txt`, 193 story words + CTA C). **Mode:** Explainer (money math), told as a story.
**Search phrase:** "thirty million now or one dollar every second". It goes in the first spoken sentence, in the
**on-screen title for the first 2 s** (`$30 MILLION NOW` / `or $1 EVERY SECOND?`, white + gold, top band, engine overlay),
and in caption line 1.
**Cast:** **Doug** = `hoodie` (takes the $30M) · **Gary** = `oxford` + glasses, `phone:true` (takes $1 a second) ·
**Victor** = `suit` (the billionaire, only in the office). Nobody talks; mouths stay still.
**The clue:** Gary's phone counter, ticking one dollar every second. It's on screen at `phone` and `day1`, before the
narrator says what it means, and it pays off at "So, the phone."
**All numbers are engine overlays.** The paintings have no text or numbers: blank phone screen, blank whiteboard, clock
without numbers. The engine draws the counter, the whiteboard math and the bank totals, so they're sharp and exact.
**Art:** 12 new images (6 backgrounds + 6 props) from `art-prompts.md` → `art/`. **OpenArt inserts:** none needed.
**Stitch:** the video stands on its own. For TikTok, Brian also posts a stitch version: the first 3–5 s of
[@eaglewiggle2d's video](https://www.tiktok.com/@eaglewiggle2d/video/7708774068136283422), then ours.
