DEPTH: always three layers plus air: a foreground element cropped by the frame edge, the stage and hero in the middle, a deep background, then atmosphere (haze, dust in light shafts, glow, caustic water light). Distant things get lighter, bluer and softer.
LIGHT: cinematic and motivated. A clear key light from the direction I give, plus practical light sources inside the scene (tank glow, lamps, screens, windows) with colored glow, warm-cool contrast, colored shadows (blue or purple, never gray or black), rim light on edges, reflections on glossy floors.
COLOR: a harmonious palette with one strong accent color, as I specify per image.
TEXTURE: every surface has a material: thick glass with reflections, polished concrete, brushed steel, rivets, wood grain, rope, painted metal, parchment, water.
COMPOSITION: portrait 2:3. Leave the open floor zone I describe clear and flat for characters to stand (texture and reflections are fine, objects are not). Keep the band at 60-72% of the height, in the center, calm and low-contrast (captions go there). Keep the top 7% and bottom 20% simple.
STRICT: no people, no characters, no faces, no hands, no feet, no silhouettes of people. NO FISH OR SHARKS OR ANY ANIMALS in backgrounds (the tanks are empty: water, rocks, kelp, bubbles only; I add the animals later). No numbers anywhere. HOW TO WORK: I'll send prompts in numbered batches. Make every image in the batch one after another, in order, portrait 2:3. Above each image write only its file name. Props go on a solid pure green #00FF00 background with no shadow on the background. When a batch is done, say "BATCH DONE".
