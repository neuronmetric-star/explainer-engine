# Sources: Why can't dog people and cat people agree? (cats vs dogs, pros and cons)

**Doug, Gary, Biscuit (the dog) and Kevin (the cat) are fictional**, and so are the lunch sprint and the late shift.
The facts about dogs and cats are real. General information about how pets work, not advice for any one household.

## Checked and left out
- **Lifespan** (cats live longer?): sources conflict. A Banfield analysis of US vet records found life expectancy at birth of 12.7 years for dogs vs 11.2 for cats, while popular sites claim the reverse. Not used. https://www.banfieldexchange.com/News/Quantification-of-life-expectancy
- **The Vegas weekend** (an earlier draft): leaving a cat alone for two days goes against vet guidance, so it became a long workday.
