# Sources: Dogs vs cats (Brian's pet-swap script)

**The whole story is fiction**: Doug, Gary, Biscuit (dog), Kevin (cat), the Saturday swap, the rain, the sandwich and the
lap nap. It makes no statistics claims. The pet behavior it shows is ordinary and true to life:

| Moment in the script | Real-world basis |
|---|---|
| Biscuit needs to go out to pee, several times a day ("By lunch, Gary's taken Biscuit out three times") | AKC (chief vet Dr. Jerry Klein): healthy adult dogs relieve themselves about 3–5 times a day: https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/health/why-is-my-dog-peeing-so-much-urinary-frequency/ · Purina UK: https://www.purina.co.uk/articles/dogs/behaviour/common-questions/how-often-do-dogs-need-to-pee |
| "Kevin's got food, water, and a litter box" (Doug can sleep in) | Healthy adult cats can generally be left 8–12 hours with food, water and a litter box. PetMD: https://www.petmd.com/cat/general-health/how-long-can-you-leave-a-cat-alone |
| Kevin looks at Doug, walks away; ignores "Kevin!" | Cats recognize their own names but often don't respond. Saito et al. 2019, *Scientific Reports*: https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/focus/en/press/z0508_00040.html |
| Biscuit "greets him like he's been gone for a year" | Dogs greet owners with more tail wagging and contact after longer absences. Rehn & Keeling 2011: https://pub.epsilon.slu.se/id/document/9061 |
| Biscuit eats the dropped sandwich | **Comedy beat, not advice.** Some sandwich ingredients (onion, garlic, grapes/raisins) are toxic to dogs. ASPCA: https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/animal-poison-control/people-foods-avoid-feeding-your-pets |

The fact-heavy first draft (walks, costs, alone time, name study) is in `script-v1-claude.txt` + `sources-v1-claude.md`.
