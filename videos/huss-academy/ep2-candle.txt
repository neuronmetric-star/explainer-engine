[#flame] A flame isn't the wax burning. The wax melts, rises up the wick, and turns into a gas. The gas is what burns.
[#need] So a flame needs two things. Hot gas, right at the wick. [#heat] And enough heat to keep it going.
[#puff] A quick puff does both jobs at once. It blows the hot gas away from the wick, and it cools what's left. [#dead] No gas, no heat, no flame.
[#camp] A campfire is different. It has a giant pile of hot wood and hot coals. Your breath can't move that much heat. [#more] Blowing on it actually feeds it, because air brings fresh oxygen.
[#bet] That's why a tiny flame goes out and a big one grows.
[#smoke] And the smoke after the candle? That's wax gas that didn't burn yet. Light it with a match, and the flame jumps back down.
