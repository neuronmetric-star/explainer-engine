# Sources — "How many eggs does $10 buy?" (draft, 15 Nov 2026)

Item tracked: one dozen Grade A large eggs (BLS series APU0000708111, U.S. city average; series starts 1980).
Dozens bought = floor($10 / price).

Wages (federal minimum):
- 1950: $0.75/hour (FLSA 1949 amendments, effective Jan 1950) — https://calcmatic.pages.dev/articles/grocery-prices-then-vs-now/ ⚠ confirm on dol.gov
- 2026: $7.25/hour (unchanged since 2009) ⚠ confirm on dol.gov
- $10 ÷ $0.75 = 13.3 h; $10 ÷ $7.25 = 1.38 h ≈ 1 h 23 min.
- One hour buys: 1950 1.25 dozen ($0.75/$0.60); 2026 3.1 dozen ($7.25/$2.32).
