# Art prompts: want-what-others-have (18 start frames for OpenArt)

**How to use:** open a **new ChatGPT chat**. Attach `ref/cast_sheet.jpg` (the four characters).
Paste everything from START down to "BATCH 1", then send each batch when ChatGPT says BATCH DONE.
Save the images with these names into `videos/want-what-others-have/art/` (or just send them to me in the chat).

**Different from our usual set:** these are **start frames for AI video**, so the characters ARE drawn in.
If a character drifts off-model, re-attach `cast_sheet.jpg` with that prompt.

```
START
You are the background artist for a vertical animated explainer series. For every image I ask for, follow this style bible exactly:

STYLE: flat 2D storybook illustration for a modern animated show. Clean, simple shapes with soft painterly gradients, subtle paper grain, bold readable silhouettes, slightly exaggerated cartoon proportions (chunky buildings, rounded trees, oversized props). No outlines on the background, or very thin darker outlines only.
COLOR & LIGHT: rich, saturated but harmonious colors. Always a clear light direction: warm light on lit sides, cool blue or purple in shadows. Gentle atmospheric haze that makes distant things lighter and bluer. Dark scenes are deep blue, never pure black.
COMPOSITION: portrait 2:3. Keep all the important action inside the middle 80% of the width (the sides get cropped to 9:16). Keep the top 10% simple. Strong depth: foreground, main subject in the middle, background far away.
STRICT: no text, letters, numbers, logos or brand badges anywhere (no badges on cars, no street signs with words).

THIS VIDEO IS DIFFERENT: these images are start frames for an animated short, so they DO include characters. Draw ONLY the characters I name, exactly like the attached cast sheet, in the same flat 2D painted style as the backgrounds:
- Every character: big glossy WHITE sphere head, a simple drawn face (two dot eyes, small eyebrows, a thin line mouth, mouth always CLOSED), a short black stick neck, white cartoon gloves for hands, no ears, no nose, no hair.
- DOUG (cast sheet, 1st): mustard-yellow hoodie with white drawstrings, light blue jeans, white sneakers.
- GARY (2nd): pale blue button-up oxford shirt, brown belt, khakis, black rectangular glasses.
- VICTOR (3rd): stocky, navy suit, white shirt, red tie, gold watch.
- POLO GUY (4th): olive-green polo shirt, khakis, gray sneakers, black watch.
- WALT (not on the sheet): same sphere-head style, gray cardigan over a white shirt, teal scarf, dark trousers, sitting in a simple modern manual wheelchair. Calm and dignified.
No other people anywhere (empty sidewalks, empty cars except the drivers named).

LOCKED OBJECTS (identical in every image, never redesign them):
- THE YELLOW SUPERCAR: a low, wide, sharp wedge-shaped Italian-style supercar, bright glossy yellow, black side air intakes, thin angular headlights, black five-spoke wheels, NO logos.
- THE BLACK SUV: a tall, boxy, square luxury SUV, glossy black, round headlights, spare tire on the back door, chrome trim, NO logos.
- THE LITTLE CAR: a small, round, slightly dented teal hatchback with one mismatched gray door.
- THE HELICOPTER: a sleek private helicopter, white with a navy stripe, tinted windows, NO logos or numbers.
- VICTOR'S OFFICE: a small cramped office at night, one desk lamp with warm light, piles of papers, an old computer, a dark window with city lights.
- THOUGHT BUBBLES: classic white cartoon thought bubble (a cloud shape with two small circles leading down to the head), soft shadow.
- THE INTERSECTION: a clean city intersection at golden hour, warm sun low from the LEFT, a hanging traffic light, glass and brick buildings, a few round trees, a bus stop with a bench on the right sidewalk.

HOW TO WORK: I'll send prompts in numbered batches. Make every image in the batch one after another, in order, portrait 2:3. Above each image write only its file name. When a batch is done, say "BATCH DONE".
Reply only with the images.

BATCH 1 (F01–F05)

THE YELLOW SUPERCAR is first in line in the middle lane, facing the camera at a slight angle; VICTOR sits behind the wheel, visible through the windshield, proud smile. THE BLACK SUV waits in the next lane on the left, GARY at the wheel. THE LITTLE CAR waits behind them, POLO GUY at the wheel. Warm sun from the left, long purple shadows.
