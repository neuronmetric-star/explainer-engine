# Sources — The Man Who Bought (Almost) Every Lottery Ticket

- International Lotto Fund v. Virginia Lottery Dept., 800 F. Supp. 337 (E.D. Va. 1992) — https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp/800/337/1393432/
  Feb 15, 1992 drawing; the Fund won the jackpot of $27,036,142 (+ $662,868 in smaller prizes).
- UPI, "Lotto-cornering syndicate gets $27 million" (Mar 10, 1992) — https://www.upi.com/Archives/1992/03/10/Lotto-cornering-syndicate-gets-27-million/9735700203600/
  International Lotto Fund of Melbourne bought ~5 million of the ~7.1 million combinations at $1 each; ran out of time; lottery decided to pay; 20 annual payments.
- Washington Post, "Australians Luck Out in Va. Lottery" (Mar 10, 1992) — https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1992/03/10/australians-luck-out-in-va-lottery/cbbfbd0c-0c7d-4faa-bf55-95bd6590dc70/
  At least 5.6 million tickets; Stefan Mandel acknowledged he was behind the scheme; rules on block purchases tightened.
- Washington Post, "Va. Lottery May Invalidate $27 Million Winning Ticket" (Mar 3, 1992) — https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/1992/03/03/va-lottery-may-invalidate-27-million-winning-ticket/a151c9e7-d0ee-45a7-b3c1-c78aa9d549a4/
  Many tickets paid for at Farm Fresh grocery chain HQ in Norfolk; lottery considered not paying.
- Washington Post, "Va. Lottery Puts Off Tightening Rules as Ticket-Buying Mystery Deepens" (Feb 25, 1992) — https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/1992/02/25/va-lottery-puts-off-tightening-rules-as-ticket-buying-mystery-deepens/88304449-b5eb-4a59-bf42-e06d3a9a2d90/
  Officials puzzled by the mass ticket buying.
- Tampa Bay Times, "Va. investigates lottery winners" (Mar 7, 1992) — https://www.tampabay.com/archive/1992/03/07/va-investigates-lottery-winners/
  Virginia State Police and FBI investigating.
- Math: Virginia Lotto was 6 of 44 → C(44,6) = 7,059,052 combinations.
