# Voice direction: high-imagine

**Paste the CLEAN copy at the bottom into the recorder** (no tags). Drama by punctuation: `…` = pause, `!` = energy, short lines = slower.
Mood: same as ep. 1. The narrator is the sober friend telling the story, dry and amused. "Yo bro… imagine" = slow, dreamy, a big lazy smile.
Every answer ("Bad news." / "You could.") snaps back to normal speed, like a fact check. "They crack up" = say it smiling, almost laughing.
Biggest moment: "Yo bro… imagine we just went to sleep." (slow) … "Nah." (instant, flat).
