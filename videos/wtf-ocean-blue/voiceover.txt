Why the fuck is the ocean blue if water is clear? It's not reflecting the sky. Water itself is a little bit blue. Here's how it works. Sunlight is every color mixed together: red, orange, yellow, green, blue. Step one: water eats red. Water molecules absorb red light first. Sam dives with red fins. At just five meters, about ninety percent of the red is gone. His fins look brown. Step two: the rest fades. Go deeper, and orange, yellow, then green get swallowed too. Step three: blue survives. Blue travels the farthest, bounces around, and comes back up to your eye. There's just one exception. Near the coast, plankton and mud turn water green or brown. Below two hundred meters, hardly any light. Below one thousand, total darkness. A glass of water is about ten centimeters deep. The ocean averages about three thousand seven hundred meters.
