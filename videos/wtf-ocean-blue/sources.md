# Sources — Why the fuck is the ocean blue if water is clear?
- **Why is the ocean blue?** — NOAA Ocean Service — https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/oceanblue.html — Water absorbs red part of spectrum, leaving blue; sediments/particles change hue; hardly any light below 200 m (656 ft), none below 1,000 m (3,280 ft).
- **Why is the ocean blue?** — Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution — https://www.whoi.edu/know-your-ocean/did-you-know/why-is-the-ocean-blue — Sky reflection is minor; blue comes from water itself; phytoplankton make water greener.
- **Why Is the Ocean Blue?** — Live Science — https://livescience.com/44205-why-is-the-ocean-blue.html — "Reflects the sky" is a misconception; blue penetrates deepest (NASA).
- **How to get awesome color in underwater photos** — Scuba Diving magazine — https://www.scubadiving.com/how-get-awesome-color-your-underwater-photos — At 5 m (15 ft), ~90% of red is gone.
- **How deep is the ocean?** — NOAA Ocean Service — https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/oceandepth.html — Average ocean depth ≈ 3,682 m (12,100 ft).
