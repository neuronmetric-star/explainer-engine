Why the f*ck is the ocean blue if water is clear? 🌊💙

It's NOT reflecting the sky. Water eats red light first. At just 5 meters, about 90% of red is gone. Blue goes the farthest and bounces back to your eye.

Your glass is ~10 cm deep. The ocean averages ~3,700 m. That's 37,000× more water. 🤯
