Why the fuck is the ocean blue if water is clear? It's not reflecting the sky. Water itself is a little bit blue. Here's how it works. Sunlight is every color mixed together: red, orange, yellow, green, blue. Step one: water eats red. Water molecules absorb red light first. Sam dives with red fins. At just 5 meters, about 90% of the red is gone. His fins look brown. Step two: the rest fades. Go deeper, and orange, yellow, then green get swallowed too. Step three: blue survives. Blue travels the farthest, bounces around, and comes back up to your eye. There's just one exception. Near the coast, plankton and mud turn water green or brown. Below 200 meters, hardly any light. Below 1,000, total darkness. A glass of water is about 10 cm deep. The ocean averages about 3,700 meters. Water isn't clear. There's just not enough of it in your glass to see the blue.
