# Voice direction: high-conversation

**Paste the CLEAN copy at the bottom into the recorder** (no tags). Drama by punctuation: `…` = pause, `!` = energy, short lines = slower.
Mood: the narrator is the sober friend telling the story, dry and amused. Doug and Gary's lines: slow, dreamy, a little too serious
("Bro…" stretched out). Every "True." is short and dry, like a fact check, then the explanation at normal speed. Build the energy: each fact a little more mind-blown than the last. No shout (test continues).
Biggest moment: "And now… you can see yours." Slow, then a quick, flat "Sorry."
