# Sources: Why the heck does the guy who misses more arrows win? (Brian's script)

**Fable.** Doug, Gary, the three gates and the contest are fictional. Numbers are invented and add up:
Doug 10 arrows / 3 bullseyes, Gary 30 arrows / 6 bullseyes (Gary misses more and still wins 6–3).
