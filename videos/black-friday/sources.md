# Sources: Are Black Friday deals actually deals?

**Doug, Gary, the TV and its prices ($800 / $500) are fictional.** The psychology and the rules are real.
No personal financial advice: the video explains how price tags work and how to check one.

| Claim in the script | Source |
|---|---|
| The first price you see becomes an "anchor" that pulls your sense of what something is worth (anchoring) | Tversky & Kahneman, "Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases," *Science* 185 (1974): https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.185.4157.1124 · Shopify, principles of persuasion (anchoring with "was" prices): https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/44331971-6-scientific-principles-of-persuasion-all-smart-ecommerce-founders-know |
| A "Deal ends tonight" countdown makes waiting feel risky (urgency / scarcity); fake countdown timers are a known "dark pattern" | FTC staff report, "Bringing Dark Patterns to Light" (2022): https://www.ftc.gov/reports/bringing-dark-patterns-light · Radboud University thesis on deceptive countdown timers (2022): https://cs.ru.nl/masters-theses/2022/J_Tiemessen___The_time_is_ticking:_the_effect_of_deceptive_countdown_timers_on_consumers'_buying_behavior_and_experience.pdf |
| Stores can advertise genuine price cuts, and the comparison ("was") price should be a real price the item was actually sold at for a substantial period | FTC Guides Against Deceptive Pricing, 16 CFR 233.1 (former price comparisons): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/233.1 · eCFR: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-233 |
| Some Black Friday discounts are real, but many "deals" are the same price or cheaper at other times; "was" prices can be old or never charged | Which?, "Black Friday deals exposed as duds" (UK; 9 in 10 deals same price or cheaper at other times in its study): https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/black-friday-deals-exposed-as-duds-aoK6u3j2suyu · Which?, 2018 (87% not the cheapest of the year): https://www.which.co.uk/news/2018/11/87-of-black-friday-prices-arent-the-cheapest-of-the-year-which-reveals/ |
| Check the price history and compare the exact same model at other stores (price-history trackers exist, e.g. CamelCamelCamel for Amazon) | Which?, "How to check if a Black Friday deal is real": https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/black-friday/article/black-friday-deals-how-to-check-if-a-black-friday-deal-is-real-aKtJD2L0zJEt |
