# Sources: Why the heck does "four easy payments" make you buy more?

**Doug, Gary, the console, the jar, the headphones/jacket/pizza and "the same Friday" are our illustrative story.** The research
and survey numbers are real. Not advice: the script explains how pay-later plans affect what people buy; it doesn't tell anyone what to do.

| Claim in the script | Source |
|---|---|
| "buy now, pay later… splits the price into four payments, one every two weeks": "Pay in 4" plans split a purchase into four interest-free installments, typically every two weeks | CFPB report, Jan 2025, *Consumer Use of Buy Now, Pay Later and Other Unsecured Debt*: https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_BNPL_Report_2025_01.pdf · Federal Reserve explainer: https://federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-august-consumer-community-context.htm |
| "Pizza: four payments": pay-later for food delivery exists (DoorDash added Klarna's Pay in 4 at checkout, announced March 2025; $35 minimum) | DoorDash: https://about.doordash.com/en-us/news/doordash-partners-with-klarna · Axios: https://www.axios.com/2025/03/20/buy-now-pay-later-doordash-klarna-bnpl |
| "But they're all due the same Friday": holding several pay-later loans at once ("loan stacking") | CFPB Jan 2025 report (loan stacking section): https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-research-reveals-heavy-buy-now-pay-later-use-among-borrowers-with-high-credit-balances-and-multiple-pay-in-four-loans |
| "Show it 'a hundred,' and the whole thing feels like a hundred. | "At one big U.S. store, pay-later shoppers bought more often and spent more each time." | Maesen & Ang (2025), "Buy Now, Pay Later: Impact of Installment Payments on Customer Purchases," *Journal of Marketing* (DOI 10.1177/00222429241282414): transaction data from a large US retailer; BNPL adoption → higher purchase incidence and larger purchase amounts. https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/216782/ · AMA summary: https://ama.org/2025/02/04/buy-now-pay-later-increases-customer-spending · Also: Di Maggio, Katz & Williams (2022), NBER w30508, BNPL access raises total spending: https://www.nber.org/papers/w30508 |
