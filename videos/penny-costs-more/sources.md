# Sources: penny-costs-more ("Why does a penny cost more than a penny to make?") · Money Heck ep. 1

Researched Nov 25 2026 (web search). Search phrase: "why does a penny cost more than a penny".

## Fiction / reconstruction (labeled)
- Doug's lemonade stand (selling for 1¢, each cup costs ~4¢) is a teaching comparison, not a real event.
- The last-penny scene shows the press and the coin only; the real person who pressed the button (Treasurer Brandon Beach) is not portrayed by our cast.

## Facts in the script
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| A penny cost the Mint 3.69 cents to make and distribute in fiscal 2024 (up from 3.07¢) | CoinNews, "Penny costs 3.69 cents to make in 2024": https://www.coinnews.net/2025/02/10/penny-costs-3-69-cents-to-make-in-2024/ · St. Louis Fed, "When a Penny Costs More Than a Penny": https://www.stlouisfed.org/publications/page-one-economics/2026/may/penny-costs-more-than-penny-economics-behind-ending-the-cent |
