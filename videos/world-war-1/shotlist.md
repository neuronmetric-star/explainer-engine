# Shot list: "Why is it called World War 1?" (short, ≈ 1:05–1:10)

**Script:** locked (`script.txt`, approved Nov 22 2026; 191 story words + CTA C). **Mode:** True story.
**Search phrase:** "why is it called world war 1". It goes in the first spoken sentence, in the **on-screen title for
the first 2 s** (`WHY IS IT CALLED` / `WORLD WAR 1?`, white + gold, top band, engine overlay), and in caption line 1.
**Cast:** **Doug** = `hoodie` (fictional 1914 soldier; never holds a weapon) · **Gary** = `oxford` + glasses, plays
**Charles Repington**, the British war reporter (real person, silent; shown only writing the name, plus the ending gag).
Nobody talks; mouths stay still. Tone: the jokes are about the *name*, never about the dead. No blood, no guns, no explosions.
**The clue:** the word **"First"** in Repington's diary. It's written on screen at `first`, before the narrator explains
why, and pays off on the last line ("just the first").
**All text and numbers are engine overlays** (title, years, the diary handwriting, the magazine headline), except the
Wells book cover (P01, exact text). Paintings have no text: blank posters, blank magazine pages, unlabeled map.
**Art:** **12 new images** (7 backgrounds + 5 props) from `art-prompts.md` → `art/`. Already in `art/`: `H01_helicopter.png`
(the helicopter ending) and `P06_calendar_page.png` (reused from dollar-a-second).
**OpenArt inserts:** none needed (no water, fire, crowds or crashes; war action stays off screen on purpose).
