# Sources: Why is it called World War 1?

**Doug (the 1914 soldier) is fictional.** Charles Repington is real; Gary plays him silently (reconstruction: we show
only what he did, writing the name in his diary). Everything else is real history.

**Careful wording (for comments):** Repington wasn't the very first to say "first world war": German biologist Ernst
Haeckel used it in September 1914 (Wikipedia, above), and a Feb 1919 British paper ran "World War No. 2" as a warning.
The script only says Repington wrote it in his diary, which is true. Britons kept saying "the Great War" into the 1940s
(History.com, above). Before 1914, "the Great War" meant the Napoleonic Wars (HistoryNet).
