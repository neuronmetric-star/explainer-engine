What the heck?!
How does a printer work?
Zoom into any printed photo… It's not a picture… It's dots.
See, Doug had a problem. He's printing Gary's dog for his birthday. But his printer hasn't printed in three months. He was saving ink.
Honestly? Smart… He hits print… and the dog comes out striped.
Inside, a little box slides left and right over the paper. Under it: hundreds of holes, thinner than a hair. Behind each hole, a tiny heater.
Zap! It boils the ink in a blink. A bubble pops up and shoves out one drop. Like a kettle spitting. Thousands of times a second.
It never mixes colors. Just four colors of dots, side by side, and your eyes blend them. Like sprinkles on a donut.
And this really happened… In 1977, at Canon, a hot soldering iron touched a syringe of ink, by accident… The ink shot out. That accident is inside millions of printers.
Doug's stripes? In three months, the ink dried in those tiny holes, like a crusty ketchup cap. Clogged holes… missing stripes.
The cleaning button squirts ink through. It works… But it drinks ink.
Doug never printed to save ink… Then the cleaning spent the ink he saved.
Zoom your phone into any printed photo. See the dots? One word.
Still here? Then that means you enjoyed the video. So follow and like for more.
