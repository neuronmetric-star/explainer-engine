# Art prompts: printer-how-it-works

**How:** new ChatGPT chat → paste **START** → **BATCH 1** (5 backgrounds) → send Claude the images → **BATCH 2** (4 props on green).
The ending is the signature E01 city sunset (already painted).

**Color script:** B05 the photo up close (bright CMYK dots, playful) → B01 home desk (cozy evening, warm lamp + cool screen glow) →
B02 printer cutaway (clean, bright, toy-like machinery) → B03 nozzle macro (deep blue ink world, one hot orange heater) →
B04 1977 lab (warm retro beige and avocado green) → the sunset goodbye.

```
START
You are the lead background painter for a premium vertical animated series. Every image is a finished, cinematic background painting for a high-end 2D animated film, made for a 9:16 phone screen. Follow this style bible exactly:

STYLE: rich 2D storybook painting with feature-film polish. Appealing, slightly exaggerated cartoon shapes (chunky furniture, oversized props, soft rounded silhouettes), but painted with real depth and detail: layered painterly brushwork, soft gradients, subtle paper grain, crisp edges on the hero object, softer edges in the distance. Every place looks lived-in and specific, never empty or generic: dense, organized set dressing, small storytelling details, believable clutter at the edges.
DEPTH: always three layers plus air: a foreground element cropped by the frame edge, the stage and hero in the middle, a deep background, then atmosphere (haze, dust in light shafts, glow). Distant things get lighter, bluer and softer.
LIGHT: cinematic and motivated. A clear key light from the direction I give, plus practical light sources inside the scene (lamps, screens, windows) with colored glow, warm-cool contrast, colored shadows (blue or purple, never gray or black), rim light on edges, reflections on glossy surfaces.
COLOR: a harmonious palette with one strong accent color, as I specify per image.
TEXTURE: every surface has a material: wood grain, matte plastic, brushed metal, paper fibers, glass, liquid ink.
COMPOSITION: portrait 2:3. Leave the open floor zone I describe clear and flat for characters to stand (texture is fine, objects are not). Keep the band at 60-72% of the height, in the center, calm and low-contrast (captions go there). Keep the top 7% and bottom 20% simple.
STRICT: no people, no characters, no animals (except inside the printed photo when I ask), no faces, no hands. No numbers anywhere. No text, letters or logos; screens, buttons and labels stay blank or show simple pictogram icons.

RECURRING OBJECTS (identical every time they appear, never redesign them):
- THE PRINTER: a chunky, friendly home inkjet printer in matte white plastic with soft rounded corners, a light-gray lid, a slanted paper tray sticking up at the back, a pull-out output tray at the front, a small round power button with a soft blue light, and a tiny blank screen.
- THE PHOTO: a glossy printed photo with a thin white border of a happy golden retriever sitting on green grass, tongue out, under a blue sky.
- THE INKS: four ink cartridges side by side in this exact order: light blue (cyan), pink (magenta), yellow, black.

HOW TO WORK: I'll send prompts in numbered batches. Make every image in the batch one after another, in order, portrait 2:3. Above each image write only its file name. Props go on a solid pure green #00FF00 background with no shadow on the background. When a batch is done, say "BATCH DONE".
```
