# Shot list: "Why did El Salvador send thousands of troops to Haiti?" (short, ~1:35)

**Cast (3D, silent, mouths still). Stand-ins, never likenesses:**
- **Victor** = `suit`: "the president" (Bukele). Always shown as a generic boss figure; no attempt to resemble him.
- **Polo guy ×4** = `polo`: the four men (helicopter mechanics). Four instances, standing in a row. They appear only
  in the setup and the groceries beat; after "disappeared" they are shown as **four empty spots** (four blob shadows, no men).
- **Gary** = `oxford`: "groups that protect regular people" (the observer). Gary carries a clipboard on his beat.
- Doug isn't used.

**Art:** 12 backgrounds + 9 props = **21 images**, all in ChatGPT from `art-prompts.md` (4 batches). Save them, with
the names given, into `videos/salvador-haiti/art/`. Explosion burst: reuse `videos/gas-vs-water/art/P07_explosion.png` (same style).
**OpenArt video insert:** 1 (cue `rubble`), only after the art arrives, one test clip first, credits reported.
**Plants (visible before they're explained):**
- **The three conditions** (P05 clipboard with 3 empty boxes) on screen at `offer`. They pay off at `check`.
- **The four men's truck** (P02) intact at `oneday`/`ambush`. At `truck` it pays off as the burned shell painted in 10.

**Tone:** real people and real deaths. No gags on the victims, nothing gory. The strike is shown as dust and rubble, never bodies.
Haiti is painted as a real, colorful place people live in, not as a ruin. Flags are plain stripes with no emblems.
