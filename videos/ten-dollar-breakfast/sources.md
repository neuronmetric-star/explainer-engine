# Sources: "How much breakfast does $10 buy?" (draft, 16 Nov 2026)

**Basket:** 1 lb white bread + 1 dozen Grade A large eggs + 1 gallon whole milk.
**Bags** = floor($10 ÷ basket). Values from 1980 on come from BLS average-price series (APU0000702111 bread, APU0000708111 eggs, APU0000709112 milk), read through sites that republish them. Values before 1980 come from secondary compilations that cite BLS. ⚠ = verify against the primary source before publishing. bls.gov, FRED and FRASER were blocked from this environment.

| Year | Bread/lb | Eggs/doz | Milk/gal | Basket | $10 buys |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1950 | $0.14 ⚠ | $0.60 ⚠ | $0.83 ⚠ | $1.57 | 6 |
| 1960 | $0.20 ⚠ | $0.57 ⚠ | $1.04 ⚠ | $1.81 | 5 |
| 1970 | $0.24 ⚠ (other sources: $0.28) | $0.61 ⚠ | $1.32 ⚠ (one source: $0.95) | $2.17 | 4 |
| 1980 | $0.51 | $0.84 | ~$2.00 ⚠ (West half-gallon ×2: $1.90–2.08) | $3.35 | 2 (2.99) |
| 1990 | $0.69 | $1.01 | ~$2.50 ⚠ (West ×2: $2.51–2.66; survey $2.38–2.52) | $4.20 | 2 |
| 2000 | $0.93 | $0.91 | $2.78 | $4.62 | 2 |
| 2010 | $1.37 | $1.66 | $3.26 | $6.29 | 1 |
| 2020 | $1.45 | $1.51 | $3.32 | $6.28 | 1 |
| Oct 2026 | $1.86 ⚠ (Jul: $1.82) | $2.32 | $4.10 | $8.28 | 1 (+$1.72 change) |

**Fragile points:** the 1980 count (2.99, so "can't even afford 3" depends on the milk estimate) and the 1970 count (4 or 5, depending on which bread and milk figures are used). Verify both.
