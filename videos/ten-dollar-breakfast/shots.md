# Shot plan: ten-dollar breakfast (3D, 9:16, PixVerse V6 720p)

## Consistency locks (in every keyframe and every clip prompt)

- **RAY:** always from the character sheet. White ball head with drawn face, no ears; dark-brown flat cap; mustard-yellow 4-button cardigan over a white shirt; navy trousers; brown shoes. Never changes clothes across decades.
- **PROPS:** always from the prop sheet. Same bread loaf (red twist tie), grey egg carton, white gallon jug (red cap), brown paper bag, green $10 bill.
- **ONE STORE SET:** the same corner grocery from the same camera angle. A checkout counter on the left, Ray in the centre, a wall of shelves behind him, and a window on the right showing the street.
  - Only the era dressing changes: wall color, register model, the car outside the window, lights.
  - The layout, counter position and window position never move.
  - Every store keyframe uses the 1950 store keyframe as "THE PLACE".
- **BAG LINE-UP:** the bags stand in one row on the counter, left to right, so the count is readable at a glance. The count shown must equal the line.
- **CAMERA:** the store shots repeat one framing (medium-wide, eye level) with a slow push-in, so the decades feel like the same place changing.
- **COLOR MEANING:** green = cash ($10 bill), red = price-jump moments, gold = the "catch" reveal.
- **CASHIER:** a background stickman cashier behind the counter in every store shot, a woman with a simple green apron, always the same.
