Why the f*ck do we dream, and why do we forget them? 💭🧠

You dream about 2 hours a night. Your brain is working the night shift: replaying the day, rehearsing threats, deleting junk.

But your memory saver (the hippocampus) is one of the last parts to wake up. So the dream never gets saved. 💾❌
