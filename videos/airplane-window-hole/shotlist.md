# Shot list: "Why the heck do airplane windows have a tiny hole?" (short, ≈ 1:08–1:12)

**Script:** approved Nov 23 2026 (`script.txt`, ≈ 202 story words + CTA C; altitude lines made plainer at Brian's request).
**Mode:** Explainer, build-break-fix chain (PLAYBOOK §1.7a).
**Search phrase:** "why do airplane windows have a hole". **On-screen title for the first 2 s** (engine overlay, top band):
`WHY DO AIRPLANE WINDOWS` (white) / `HAVE A HOLE?` (gold). The phrase is also caption line 1 in `post.md`.
**Series banner (every video from now on):** small pill `THE HECK?` at the top center (y ≈ 150–200), the whole video, calm style
(white text on a dark rounded pill), drawn by the engine. The 2-second title sits under it.
**Color code (every video):** white arrows = air / force · light blue = water / moisture · red = crack / danger / loss ·
gold = the answer (the hole) · gold dashed line = normal level. Captions keep gold = key word, red = loss.
**Cast** (nobody talks; mouths still; the narrator performs Victor's one line in his own voice):
- **Doug** = `{outfit:'hoodie', hat:'hard'}`; `holdR:'drill'` only in the drill shots: builds the windows. Confident, gets it half right.
- **Victor** = `{outfit:'suit', hat:'inspector', holdL:'clipboard'}`: the inspector. Asks "And if it cracks?" by gesture (`point` at the pane, `tilt`).
- **Gary** = `oxford` + glasses: the passenger in the window seat. The observer of the fog.

**The clue:** the window fogging and frosting (05 → 06). **The hole** is the payoff object: one tiny dark dot near the bottom
of the backup pane, drawn by the engine (dark center + white rim highlight), so it appears only after `drill` (and in the hook).
**The two names stay locked on screen too:** engine labels `OUTSIDE PANE` and `BACKUP PANE` (small white tags with a thin line)
on the cutaway plate (04) the first time each is named; same words every time.
**Engine overlays (no numbers in the art):** `7 MILES UP` (sky), `≈ 8 LB` on each milk jug + `PER SQUARE INCH`, `−67°F` (cold),
air arrows (white = push), a tiny swirl of damp air (light-blue wisps) leaving through the hole, a red crack line at `crack`.
**Art (standard v2, PLAYBOOK §4):** 7 backgrounds + 6 props from `art-prompts.md` → `art/` (4 batches; batch 1 is reviewed first),
plus `H01_helicopter.png` copied from `docs/assets/`.
**Color script:** 01 cabin morning (cream, teal seats, soft sky blue) → 02 Doug's workshop (warm amber, confident) → 03 the sky
diagram (deep cobalt, icy white) → 04 cutaway diagram (warm cabin side vs icy blue sky side) → 05 window seat (bright, hopeful)
→ 06 same window frosted (cold gray-blue, the failure) → 07 tarmac at sunset (warm gold return, open sky for the helicopter).
**Angles:** 01 eye-level down the aisle · 02 low 3/4 over the workbench · 03 straight side-on diagram · 04 straight-on cutaway ·
05/06 close over the seat · 07 low wide from the tarmac.
**OpenArt:** none needed (fog, frost and air arrows are simple engine overlays).
**Pace (new rule):** something changes every ≈ 2 s: inside long cues add a punch-in, a prop arriving or a gesture.
**Growing sets (new rule):** 02 workshop and 04 cutaway build up over the video instead of resetting: panes slot in, the sealant bead
stays, the hole stays, the arrows and labels add up. Each return to 02/04 shows everything added so far.
