# Sources: high-doubts

Doug, Gary, the couch and their doubts: invented (comedy skit). Every doubt is answered with real proof; no myth is presented as fact:

| Line | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| People went to the Moon: they left mirrors, scientists still shoot lasers at them today | Apollo 11, 14 and 15 left retroreflector arrays; the APOLLO station at Apache Point Observatory (New Mexico, run with NASA/UC San Diego) ranges lasers off them several times a month, to millimeter precision | [NASA Space Geodesy: Apache Point](https://earth.gsfc.nasa.gov/geo/networks/sgp/sites/apache-point) · [UC San Diego APOLLO project](https://tmurphy.physics.ucsd.edu/apollo/) |
| Regular workers built the pyramids; we found one guy's diary (Merer) logging every boat of stone | Diary of Merer (Wadi al-Jarf papyri, found 2013 by Pierre Tallet): logbook of inspector Merer's crew shipping limestone from Tura to Giza for Khufu's Great Pyramid, ≈ 4,500 years old (Egyptian Museum, Cairo) | [National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/premium/article/red-sea-scrolls-pyramids-egypt) · [History.com](https://www.history.com/news/egypts-oldest-papyri-detail-great-pyramid-construction) · [Wikipedia: Diary of Merer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diary_of_Merer) |
| They slid the big blocks on wet sand | Univ. of Amsterdam (Fall, Bonn et al., Physical Review Letters 2014): the right amount of water roughly halves the pulling force on a sledge; tomb painting of Djehutihotep shows a man pouring water in front of a sledge | [University of Amsterdam](https://iop.uva.nl/content/news/2014/00/prl-egyptian-pyramids.html) · [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/45285-how-egyptians-moved-pyramid-stones.html) |
| Dinosaurs are real; T. rex's closest living cousin is the chicken | Protein (collagen) from a 68-million-year-old T. rex femur grouped most closely with chickens and ostriches (Organ et al., Science 2008); birds are living dinosaurs | [Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2008/04/molecular-analysis-confirms-t-rexs-evolutionary-link-to-birds/) · [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/t-rex-linked-to-chickens-ostriches-180940877/) · [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2008/04/25/89942780/t-rex-protein-evidence-links-dinosaurs-to-birds) |
| A Greek guy measured how big around the Earth is with sticks and shadows, 2,000+ years ago | Eratosthenes (≈ 240 BC) compared noon shadows at Syene and Alexandria to estimate Earth's circumference, close to the real value | [APS News: Eratosthenes Measures the Earth](https://www.aps.org/apsnews/2006/06/eratosthenes-measures-earth) · [Bucknell Astronomy](https://www.eg.bucknell.edu/physics/astronomy/astr101/specials/eratosthenes.html) |
