# Voice direction: high-doubts

**Paste the CLEAN copy at the bottom into the recorder** (no tags). Drama by punctuation: `…` = pause, `!` = energy, short lines = slower.
Mood: the narrator is the sober friend. The DOUBTER is suspicious and squinty, slow ("Bro… did people actually go to the Moon? That's cap.").
"Yo bro, that's true." = confident, a little smug, then the proof at normal speed, like he's dropping a fact bomb.
"Oh no. I got a better one." = excited, competitive. "They crack up" = say it smiling, almost laughing.
Biggest moment: "T. rex's closest living cousin… is the chicken." Pause before "chicken". Then "You ate a dinosaur today." deadpan.
