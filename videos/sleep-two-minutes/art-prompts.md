**How:** new ChatGPT chat → paste **START** → then **BATCH 1** (4 backgrounds) → send Claude the images → Claude checks them →
**BATCH 2** (4 props on green). The ending is the series' signature E01 city sunset (already painted, nothing to do).

**Color script:** B01 bedroom at night (deep blue + one warm amber lamp, cozy but restless) → B02 head cutaway (warm room with a red

STYLE: rich 2D storybook painting with feature-film polish. Appealing, slightly exaggerated cartoon shapes (chunky furniture, oversized props, soft rounded silhouettes), but painted with real depth and detail: layered painterly brushwork, soft gradients, subtle paper grain, crisp edges on the hero object, softer edges in the distance. Every place looks lived-in and specific, never empty or generic: dense, organized set dressing, small storytelling details, believable clutter at the edges.
DEPTH: always three layers plus air: a foreground element cropped by the frame edge, the stage and hero in the middle, a deep background, then atmosphere (haze, dust in light shafts, glow). Distant things get lighter, bluer and softer.
LIGHT: cinematic and motivated. A clear key light from the direction I give, plus practical light sources inside the scene (lamps, screens, windows) with colored glow, warm-cool contrast, colored shadows (blue or purple, never gray or black), rim light on edges. Night is deep blue, never pure black.
COLOR: a harmonious palette with one strong accent color, as I specify per image.
TEXTURE: every surface has a material: wood grain, fabric weave, knitted wool, painted plaster, glass reflections, water.
COMPOSITION: portrait 2:3. Leave the open floor zone I describe clear and flat for characters to stand or sit (texture is fine, objects are not). Keep the band at 60-72% of the height, in the center, calm and low-contrast (captions go there). Keep the top 7% and bottom 20% simple.
STRICT: no people, no characters, no animals, no faces, no hands, no feet, no silhouettes of people. No numbers anywhere (clocks have tick marks only). No text, letters or logos; signs, screens and labels stay blank or show simple pictogram icons.

RECURRING OBJECTS (identical every time they appear, never redesign them):
- THE BED: a chunky wooden bed with a rounded headboard, a navy-blue duvet with a thin mustard stripe, two plump white pillows, rumpled like someone tossed and turned.
- THE ARMCHAIR: a big round-backed armchair in mustard-yellow velvet with a knitted gray throw blanket over one arm.
- THE LAMP: a brass reading lamp with a cream fabric shade, switched ON, glowing warm amber.
- THE WINDOW: a tall window with dark blue curtains half open, a big pale moon and city lights outside.

HOW TO WORK: I'll send prompts in numbered batches. Make every image in the batch one after another, in order, portrait 2:3. Above each image write only its file name. Props go on a solid pure green #00FF00 background with no shadow on the background. When a batch is done, say "BATCH DONE".
