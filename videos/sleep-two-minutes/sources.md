# Sources: sleep-two-minutes ("How do you fall asleep in 2 minutes?") · Body Heck episode 1

Researched Nov 24 2026 (web search). Search phrase: "how to fall asleep in 2 minutes" (also "military sleep method").
Reference that inspired it: an "Explained in 3D" TikTok (345.7K likes, 119.4K saves) about the US Navy pilots' sleep trick.

## Fiction / reconstruction (labeled)
- Doug and Gary, the big day, the ceiling-staring, the guard dog in the brain: **invented characters and a teaching analogy**.
- The canoe on a calm lake is one of the pictures Winter's method uses; the guard dog is our comparison for "a tense body keeps you alert".

## Careful wording (myth vs fact)
- The **96% in 6 weeks / 2 minutes** figure is **Bud Winter's own account** in his book, not a controlled study. No peer-reviewed trial
  has tested the exact method. So the script says **"He wrote that…"** and adds that scientists never proved the two minutes.
- What IS supported: relaxing your muscles on purpose (progressive muscle relaxation) and calm pictures help many people fall asleep
  faster. We explain how the trick works; it is not medical advice (no "this cures insomnia").
