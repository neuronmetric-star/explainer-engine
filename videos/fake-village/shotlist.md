# Shot list: "Who the heck lives in North Korea's nicest village?" (short, ~1:05)

**Script:** `script.txt` (190 story words + CTA). **Cast:**
- **Doug** = `hoodie`: the tourist who falls for it (rent gag).
- **Gary** = `oxford`: the buddy who notices the windows.
- **Polo guy** = `polo`: one of the village caretakers sweeping the empty street (a **reconstruction**: real
  caretakers are seen from afar, we don't know who they are).

**Art:** 13 new images (8 backgrounds + 5 props) from `art-prompts.md` → `videos/fake-village/art/`.
**OpenArt video inserts:** none needed (no water, fire or crowds). The lights blinking on a timer and the
binocular view are done in the engine.
**The clue:** the windows. In 04 they are dark square holes (visible in `kijong`, before Gary says "look at the
windows"); 06 shows them up close with no glass and sky straight through. Second clue: every light in 05 comes on at
the same instant (`lights`), paid off by "On a timer".
**Locked object:** THE VILLAGE (cream and pale-mint blocks, sky-blue roofs, one taller tower in the middle, tall
flagpole with a plain red flag on the right). Defined in 04, every later prompt references 04.
