# Shot list: "Why the heck did a whole town catch the dancing plague of 1518?" (trend ride, ≈ 1:10–1:13)

**Script:** approved Nov 22 2026 (`script.txt`, 215 words incl. viewer question + CTA C). **Mode:** Trend ride (PLAYBOOK §1.13).
**Meme:** "In this town, everybody goes out partying… except me. Huss, huss, huss." (@larpniverse Roblox clip; "huss" = the
hard-breathing grind sound). **Real sound:** the 4 s original replaces the narrator from [#town] to [#huss] (splice like full-price).
**Search phrase:** "dancing plague 1518" → on-screen title for the first 2 s: `DANCING PLAGUE 1518` (white) / `HUSS HUSS HUSS` (gold).
**Cast** (big on screen, PLAYBOOK §3.1; mitt hands; nobody talks):
- **Doug** = `hoodie` + a **basketball** (prop P01 at his hand, bouncing on the beat): the "except me" grinder who stays home.
- **Gary** = `oxford`, **Victor** = `suit`, **polo guy** = `polo`: the town that goes out partying, then the 1518 dancers (dance loop
  from full-price). They stand in for the townspeople (fiction, labeled); **Frau Troffea is never shown as a person**: her dance
  is a pair of red shoes (P02) dancing alone on the cobbles.
**The clue:** the red shoes. On screen from [#troffea], they come back on the dancers' feet at [#shrine] ("red shoes").
**Color script:** 01–02 modern town at night (party neon pink/purple vs. Doug's cold blue driveway) → 03 1518 street in hot July sun
(gold/ochre) → 04 the stage in the square (festive red/gold) → 05 same square at dusk, wrecked (bruised purple) → 06 road to the
shrine at dawn (pale rose/blue, calm) → 07 parchment map (sepia + red ink) → back to 02 at dusk for the ending (open sky).
**Angles:** 01 high wide over the town · 02 low from the driveway · 03 eye-level street, deep perspective · 04 wide square ·
05 same as 04 · 06 low wide road + sky · 07 straight-on diagram.
**Text overlays (engine):** `1518`, `STRASBOURG`, `~400 DANCERS`, `"HOT BLOOD"`, `CURE: MORE DANCING`, `DEATHS? DISPUTED`,
`MUSIC BANNED`, `FAMINE + DISEASE + FEAR`, viewer question card `Would you have danced, or stayed home?`.
