# Sources (bread-ladder). Searched Nov 26 2026.
- Campden BRI, "Bread dough in iron molds being baked and rising in oven" (https://www.campdenbri.co.uk/blogs/bread-dough-rise-causes.php): yeast makes equal amounts of CO2 and ethanol; heat expands gas, ethanol evaporates, steam adds to oven spring; heat kills yeast. Note: CO2 alone doesn't explain the volume jump, so the script says "gas and steam".
- Modernist Cuisine, "The Role of Yeast in Bread" (https://modernistcuisine.com/mb/the-role-of-yeast-in-bread/): yeast ferments flour sugars.
- Modernist Cuisine, "Gluten: How Does It Work?" (https://modernistcuisine.com/mcah/gluten-how-does-it-work/): gluten network traps gas; kneading builds it.
- Science Focus, "What is the yeast doing inside my bread?" (https://www.sciencefocus.com/science/what-is-the-yeast-doing-inside-my-bread/): yeast eats sugar, gives off CO2 (same gas as in fizzy drinks).
- Exploratorium, "Science of Cooking: bread" (https://annex.exploratorium.edu/cooking/bread/bread_science.html): dough structure, gluten.
Not verified: "a fresh net is stiff" and "egg white" comparison are teaching analogies, not source claims; check wording before final. Yeast being a fungus: standard biology. No numbers used.
