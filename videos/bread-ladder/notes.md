## Causal chain (every link SPOKEN)
flat cracker, no holes → yeast eats sugar, burps gas (same gas as soda) → but it leaks (loose balloon) → so knead → gluten net traps it (balloon skin) → net is stiff → rest warm → yeast eats faster, net stretches → still raw → bake → gas and steam expand → heat kills yeast, net sets (egg white) → holes stay.

## Retell test
1. Doug mixed flour and water and got a flat cracker. 2. Yeast burps gas, kneading builds a net that traps it, and oven heat makes the gas and steam expand. 3. The heat kills the yeast and sets the net, so the holes stay and it's bread.
