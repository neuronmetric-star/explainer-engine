# What we learned from the creators we studied (Nov 2026)

## @richardknows22 — "tricks the rich use" (TikTok)
- 46K followers / 497K likes from ~15 videos. G-Wagon tax trick: 2.67M views; private jet: 807K.
  His earlier court-story videos got 2–9K views: **the niche switch made the account.**
- Look: black void, glossy reflective floor, hero object as glowing **gold wireframe**, faceless white
  capsule character with a gold tie ("Richard"); "normal person" is the same figure without the tie.
  Floating gold-bordered cards (PRICE counter ticking up, HIS PAYCHECK, weight tag), 1-2-3 step tracker,
  grey PROFIT vs red TAX bars, white paper receipt "THE MATH", red for the villain (tax, "ONE RULE").
- Captions: one word at a time, center-low, key words gold/red.
- Script (~62 s): hook question → "It's not a coincidence, it's a tax trick" → price → normal person vs
  Richard → steps 1-2-3 → "There's just one rule" + sarcastic joke → the math → relatable victim
  ("a nurse pays every dollar") → "If you want to learn more tricks the rich use, follow Richard."

## @playmalo.com — interactive horror "you decide" (TikTok + app)
- 126K followers / 3.6M likes. RED-EYE Part 1: 17.1M views; every part of the 10-part series 0.9–4M.
- Look: polished 3D render, faceless white mannequins with sleep masks, dark airplane cabin, cold blue
  light, first-person POV, one unsettling design (black face, huge white grin), red-flash moments,
  1–2 word uppercase captions, two on-screen choice buttons at the end.
- Script (~2 min): "Imagine you…" second person → one creepy detail per line → a RULE → slow countdown
  threat → twist that breaks the rule → "You have two choices… what would you do?" → next part opens
  with both outcomes, a new rule and a new choice. Comments + series binge = algorithm fuel.

## Shared lessons
1. Faceless, simple 3D characters a viewer recognizes instantly.
2. One dominant color language.
3. Word-by-word captions + tight voiceover with zero filler.
4. A repeatable series format so every video advertises the next.
