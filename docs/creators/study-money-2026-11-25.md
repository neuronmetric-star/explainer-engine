## @justrichit: "Why Airlines Sell More Tickets Than Seats"
- Link: https://www.tiktok.com/@justrichit/video/7707779456592530702
- Stats: 4.9M plays · 238.8K likes · 14.2K shares · 12.3K saves · 936 comments · posted 2026-11-13
- **Length:** 66 s

**Hook (first 3 s)** `[Hook]`
- Picture: a 3D plane with more passengers than seats.
- Words: "Airlines sell more tickets than there are seats on the plane, and they do it on purpose."
- A bold "they do it on purpose" statement, shown in full as on-screen text.

**Structure / open loops** `[Script]`
1. Hook.
2. Problem: no-shows mean empty seats earning $0.
3. Strategy: sell 210 tickets for 200 seats.
4. Re-hook at 0:31: "But what happens when everyone shows up?" Gate auction $300 → $500 → $800, then bumping.
5. Payoff: a balance scale, "A FEW PAYOUTS" vs "EMPTY SEATS".

**Pacing** `[Visual]`
- The 3D camera never stops (sweeps, zooms, eye-level drops). Changes about every 2–3 s, about 20–30 per minute (est.).

**Visual style** `[Visual]`
- Polished 3D, studio lighting.
- Live counters on screen (TICKETS SOLD 201 → 210, voucher $ rising).
- Coin and ping sound effects.

**Characters** `[Character emotion]`
- Generic 3D passengers.
- Emotion comes from icons over heads (clock, thermometer, calendar) and a "DENIED" stamp. Faces barely act.

**Captions** `[Visual]`
- Heavy uppercase sans-serif, bottom-center, 1–3 words, key words in yellow.

**Ending / CTA** `[Growth]`
- The scale visual lands the logic.
- The share trigger is a useful fact: bumped passengers can be owed cash (the video says up to $2,000).
- No spoken CTA was reported.

**Steal this** `[Script]`
- **Rule:** put a spoken re-hook question ("But what happens when…?") at about the halfway mark.
- End the explanation on one physical metaphor (a balance scale) instead of a sentence.

## Money: lane rules from this batch
- `[Hook]` 3 of 3 open with a statement, not a question: "they do it on purpose", a paradox, an audience callout. No "Why the heck".
- `[Script]` Use round, concrete numbers on screen (200 vs 210 seats; 50 staff × 2 cups × £1.50). Add a mid-video spoken re-hook.
- `[Visual]` Live counters and status cards keep eyes on screen. Color-code good (green) and bad (red).
- `[Character emotion]` A recurring mascot with one signature prop, plus exaggerated face changes on money gains and losses.
- `[Growth]` The biggest video (4.9M) was 66 s, with no CTA and 3D. The 21 s listicle had the best save rate. Comments were weak across the board.
- `[Scout]` Next pull: the other @justrichit "how X makes money" videos. It's the closest fit to our format.
