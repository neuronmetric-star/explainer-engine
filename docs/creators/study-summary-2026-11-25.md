# Creator study summary (2026-11-25)

**Sample:** 13 videos analyzed across Science, Money, Body and General. Only 3 cleared 1M plays: @thebuildingbox 20.3M, @unnecessaryengineering 14.8M, @justrichit 4.9M. Weight the patterns toward those three.
**Files:** `study-science-2026-11-25.md`, `study-money-2026-11-25.md`, `study-body-2026-11-25.md`, `study-general-2026-11-25.md`

## Top 5 patterns
1. `[Hook]` **Second-person stakes beat bare questions.**
   - Both 14M+ videos make the viewer the main character ("You dig a trench…", "your legs soaking wet").
   - 3 of 3 money videos open with a statement, not a question.
2. `[Script]` **The "Perfect! … Except…" ladder.**
   - Each fix creates the next problem, 5–7 rungs in about 70 s.
   - Then one final flip undoes it all (you get out of the car and get soaked).
   - This is our "flip" beat, repeated.
3. `[Hook]` **Kill the obvious answer in line 2.**
   - "Not to find a perfect spot…", "nothing to do with flexing", "it goes deeper than that."
   - Seen in 4 videos across 3 niches.
4. `[Visual]` **Something changes every 2–3 s, and the numbers live on screen.**
   - Snap-in 3D builds, live counters (TICKETS SOLD 201 → 210), day counters, green/red status cards.
5. `[Growth]` **The ending is the share trigger, not a "follow" ask.**
   - The 3 1M+ videos had no spoken CTA. They end on a wide reveal, an irony flip or a balance-scale visual.
   - A spoken comment question helps comments only: 168 for @sketchedpsychology, the most in this batch.

## What contradicts what we do now
- **"Why the heck…" every time.** The biggest hooks weren't questions. Test second-person scenario openers ("You try to…") against "Why the heck…".
- **One word at a time captions.** Every winner showed 1–6 word chunks (most often 2–4). None used single words.
- **~60 s.** Winners ran 66–71 s. That's fine, but keep it over 61 s, since TikTok's Creator Rewards only pays on videos over 1 minute.
- **Body niche.** The best anatomy clip (620K) was 20 s, silent, one continuous shot. That's the opposite of our narrated, cut-heavy style. It may need its own format.
- **Silent cast emotion: no contradiction.** Winners show emotion through bodies and props (soaked, steaming head, googly eyes, overreaction), which is exactly our cast.
