## @thebuildingbox: "How An Active Volcano Turns Into A Lava River In Your Backyard"
- Link: https://www.tiktok.com/@thebuildingbox/video/7704085245322169630
- Stats: 20.3M plays · 846.7K likes · 34.6K shares · 40.2K saves · 5.6K comments · posted 2026-11-03
- **Length:** 67 s

**Hook (first 3 s)** `[Hook]`
- Picture: a yellow excavator digs a trench from an erupting volcano down into a suburban backyard.
- Words: "You dig a downhill trench from a volcano's lava spill to your backyard and call it a lava river."
- Not a question: it's a second-person scenario ("You dig…").

**Structure / open loops** `[Script]`
- Build → problem → fix, repeated about 7 times: overflow → no exit → heat scorches plants → can't cross → nowhere to sit → waterfall → paths.
- Each fix creates the next problem, so every step opens a fresh loop.
- Ends on a wide reveal: "Congratulations! You just built a lava river in your backyard."

**Pacing** `[Visual]`
- New element every 2–4 s, about 15–30 changes per minute (est.).
- Cuts land on action verbs (dig, line, build).

**Visual style** `[Visual]`
- Clean low-poly 3D that looks like a sim game.
- Parts "snap" into place.
- Glowing orange lava against green grass and grey stone.

**Characters** `[Character emotion]`
- No characters, only machines. The viewer ("you") is the main character.

**Captions** `[Visual]`
- White bold sans-serif with a black stroke, lower third, 1–3 words at a time, synced to the voice.

**Ending / CTA** `[Growth]`
- Pull back to show the whole build, plus "Congratulations!"
- No spoken CTA. Comments get triggered by gaps the video leaves open ("what about rain?", "sulfur gas?").

**Steal this** `[Script]`
- **Rule:** chain "fix → new problem" beats so every answer opens the next question. End on a full wide reveal of the finished thing.

## @unnecessaryengineering: "Raincoats are actually useless"
- Link: https://www.tiktok.com/@unnecessaryengineering/video/7702894571469966625
- Stats: 14.8M plays · 1.3M likes · 163.9K shares · 64.3K saves · 10.7K comments · posted 2026-10-31
- **Length:** 71 s

**Hook (first 3 s)** `[Hook]`
- Picture: a low-poly character in a raincoat walking in heavy rain, legs getting soaked.
- Words: "Why do raincoats keep your body dry, but leave your legs soaking wet? Let's fix that."
- A searchable everyday-annoyance question, also shown as on-screen text.

**Structure / open loops** `[Script]`
- Fix → "Perfect! … Except…" → bigger fix, about 5 times: long coat → wheels → dome → windshield → full 4x4 car.
- Ends with an irony flip: you arrive, get out, and get soaked anyway.

**Pacing** `[Visual]`
- Changes every 2–4 s, about 15–30 per minute (est.).
- Snap-in assembly animations.

**Visual style** `[Visual]`
- Bright low-poly 3D.
- Sound effects carry the jokes: rain, clicks, splash, wiper squeak, engine.

**Characters** `[Character emotion]`
- A mannequin-like figure with dot features.
- The emotion is physical: waddling, getting sprayed, getting soaked. There are no facial expressions.

**Captions** `[Visual]`
- White bold sans-serif, centered lower-middle, 3–6 words per chunk.

**Ending / CTA** `[Growth]`
- The irony punchline is the CTA; it makes people tag friends.
- No spoken follow ask.

**Steal this** `[Script]`
- **Rule:** use the "Perfect!… Except…" ladder.
  - Escalate the fix to something absurd.
  - Then make the last line undo it all ("…and now you have to get out of the car").
- This maps directly onto our "flip" beat.

## Science: lane rules from this batch
- `[Hook]` 2 of 3 open in second person ("You dig…", "your legs"). Second-person stakes beat a bare question.
- `[Script]` 2 of 3 use a fix → new-problem ladder with "Perfect! Except…" turns, about 5–7 rungs in roughly 70 s.
- `[Visual]` The two biggest (14.8M and 20.3M plays) are clean low-poly 3D with snap-in builds. Visuals change every 2–4 s.
- `[Character emotion]` Emotion is in bodies and props (soaked legs, googly eyes, a shrug), never in dialogue. This fits our silent cast.
- `[Growth]` No video asks out loud for a follow. The ending is the share trigger: an irony flip, a full reveal, or a dry punchline.
- `[Scout]` Every length is 67–95 s, all over 60 s.
