## What they do that we don't
1. **"You" is the main character, and the object shows up in the first 2 seconds.** "If you cut a hole in your street…",
   "Those fuzzy gray lines in your cutting board are not just knife marks." Ours: "Why the heck would you bid 60 bucks
   for tickets? You'd only pay 34 in cash." Then "Doug swears…" A stranger doesn't know Doug yet, so a named
   character in second 3 is a speed bump, not a hook.
2. **A problem → fix → new problem loop every 3–4 s.** "But… so… this is a ___." Every line raises the next question,
   so nobody has a moment to scroll. Ours stack facts (MIT study → brain scan → fries analogy), which is good, but
   nothing pulls the viewer into the next line.
3. **Things the viewer touches today, or a dark, visual story.** Street drains, cutting boards, a pit under a rug.
   Ours mix lessons (habits, reading), finance, cars, celebrities and trends.
4. **One lane, one format, every post.** Infrastructure only; "X explained in Minecraft"; "Doctor, this is why…".
   TikTok learns who the video is for. Our 18 posts in 4 days covered 18 different lanes.
5. **Ending = a payoff or a "try it tonight."** "Scrape your board tonight with a spoon: if gray fuzz peels up…"
   (that line is why it got 54k saves). Ours end with a CTA plus the helicopter, about 5 s of non-story.
6. **Caption = the search phrase + niche tags** (#HowThingsWork #Engineering). Ours sometimes say only "#fyp".

## Recommendations (in order)
1. **Pick one lane for 2 weeks: "how the heck does [everyday thing] work"** (our best fit for the cast + paintings).
   Every topic is something the viewer has touched today.
2. **Line 1 = "you" + the object + the surprise.** Doug and Gary come in at line 2–3 as the ones who act it out.
3. **Write in "But… so…" links.** A new problem every 3–4 s; name each part ("This is a catch basin").
4. **End on a "check yours tonight" line.** Test dropping the helicopter + CTA on 3 videos and compare % watched to the end
   in TikTok Studio (Brian's rule, so test it, don't just drop it).
5. **2 posts a day, ≥ 3 h apart**, not 5. Each post is a test; weak tests teach TikTok we're weak.
6. **Set the bio** ("How stuff really works, explained dry. New story daily. 📚") and pin the 3 best.
7. **Never "#fyp"-only captions.** Search phrase first, 3–5 niche tags.
8. **Comment early** on @1hidden_by_design, @instinctthatkills, @thebloxyproductionsyt, @sectorbeta (same audience).

What to check in TikTok Studio (it's not in the scrape): average watch time and % who watched to the end for the
top 3 and bottom 3. If viewers leave in the first 3 s, it's the hook; if they leave in the middle, it's the pacing.
