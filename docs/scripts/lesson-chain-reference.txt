Why the heck do people want what others have?
See, Victor had a problem. He worked really hard to buy his dream car, a Lamborghini. And now he's sitting in it, stuck at a red light.
Then a helicopter flies right over him. Victor looks up and thinks, "Man, I wish I had that."
No red lights up there.
But what he doesn't know is that the guy next to him in a G-Wagon is thinking, "Man, I wish I had that Lamborghini."
But what he doesn't know is that the guy in the regular car is thinking, "Man, I wish I had that G-Wagon."
But what he doesn't know is that Doug, walking by, is thinking, "Man, I'd take any of those cars."
Honestly? I thought it ended with Doug.
See, Victor used to picture himself behind the wheel of that Lamborghini. Now he's finally in it, picturing himself in the helicopter.
The light turns green. The cars pull away.
But what Doug doesn't know is that a man in a wheelchair is watching him walk by, thinking, "I wish I could walk again."
Sometimes we get so caught up looking at what other people have that we forget to appreciate what we already have.
What's one thing you have today that you used to wish for?
