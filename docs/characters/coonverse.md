# The Coonverse — @coonknows cast (round-head cartoon animals)

Same look as our lottery video (round heads, simple line faces, pill bodies, thin noodle limbs, mitten
hands, thick outlines, flat colors, painted cinematic backgrounds) — but every character is an animal.
Coon is the host. The other animals are "roles" that come back in every video, so viewers recognize them.

## Cast
| Character | Animal | Plays | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coon** (host) | Raccoon | The one who knows — explains, reacts, appears in hook + ending | Black bandit mask, grey fur, striped tail, sharp black suit + gold tie (like the profile pic), smug half-smile |
| **The Suits** | Owls | Officials, bankers, lawyers, rule-makers | Grey suits, tiny glasses, clipboards, one raised eyebrow |
| **Fat Cats** | Cats | The rich, CEOs, billionaires | Gold chains, cigars-free, top hats or designer suits, lazy confident eyes |
| **The Crew** | Beavers | Workers, builders, regular people doing the job | Hard hats, vests, buck teeth, sweaty and hardworking |
| **Squirrels** | Squirrels | Everyday people, savers, the crowd, "the friend who plays every week" | Hoodies, 90s windbreakers, big nervous eyes |
| **The Law** | Bulldogs | Police, FBI, guards | Dark suits + sunglasses (FBI), campaign hats (troopers) |
| **Foxes** | Foxes | Scammers, villains, the loophole guys | Slick suits, narrow eyes, grin |
| **Pigeons** | Pigeons | Doubters, gossip, the laughing crowd | Flat caps, pointing and laughing |

Rule for real people: real people are never drawn with a recognizable face. Show them as the hat
silhouette (like the lottery video) or as an animal seen from behind / in shadow, and never imply an
animal's traits are the real person's (no fox = "scammer" for a real, named person).
