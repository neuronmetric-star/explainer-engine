# The Coonverse — @coonknows cast (mature painted animals)

Style (locked Nov 2026): **rich digital painting, mature anthropomorphic animals**, adult proportions
(tall, long limbs, normal-sized heads), realistic animal faces and textured fur, tailored clothes,
cinematic warm-and-cool lighting, visible brush texture. Reference: `ref/coon-main.webp` (attach it to EVERY prompt).
**Mood is picked by topic** (locked Nov 2026) — Coon + the painted style never change, only the light:
| Topic | Mood | Light / palette line to add to every prompt |
|---|---|---|
| History, old money, wars, empires, anything before ~1950 | **Historic** | `period setting, warm candle/gas-lamp light, fog, sepia and deep red tones, oil-painting feel` |
| Modern money, science, how-things-work, today's world | **Bright** | `modern setting, bright warm sunlight, blue sky, vivid saturated colors, cheerful energy` |
| Crime, scams, mysteries, investigations (any era) | **Night** | `night, moonlight and neon or desk-lamp light, deep blues with gold highlights, cinematic shadows` |

Poses:
1. coon-intro — standing facing the viewer, one hand in pocket, calm knowing half-smile, eyebrow slightly raised
2. coon-point — pointing to his left (viewer's right) with his whole arm, explaining, mouth open mid-sentence
3. coon-smirk — arms crossed, smug smirk, looking at the viewer from the corner of his eyes
4. coon-shock — leaning back, eyes wide, paws up, genuinely shocked
5. coon-think — one paw on his chin, looking up, thinking
6. coon-watch — checking his gold pocket watch, eyebrows raised (for "time" / deadlines)
7. coon-cash — fanning a stack of cash in one paw, confident grin (money moments)
8. coon-whiskey — leaning on an invisible bar with a glass of whiskey, relaxed, for the ending line "Now you know."

## Format: Coon the invisible narrator (from "brush twice")
Coon stands INSIDE the scene (dentist office, kitchen, battlefield…) and talks to the viewer; the other
characters can't see or hear him. Gags: he leans on their equipment, waves a paw in front of their face,
shrinks down to "tour" the micro-world (inside a mouth, a cell, an engine). He's always fully painted (not a ghost).
