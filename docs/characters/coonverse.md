# The Coonverse — @coonknows cast (mature painted animals)

Style (locked Nov 2026): **rich digital painting, mature anthropomorphic animals**, adult proportions
(tall, long limbs, normal-sized heads), realistic animal faces and textured fur, tailored clothes,
cinematic warm-and-cool lighting, visible brush texture. Reference: `ref/coon-main.webp` (attach it to EVERY prompt).
**Mood is picked by topic** (locked Nov 2026) — Coon + the painted style never change, only the light:
| Topic | Mood | Light / palette line to add to every prompt |
|---|---|---|
| History, old money, wars, empires, anything before ~1950 | **Historic** | `period setting, warm candle/gas-lamp light, fog, sepia and deep red tones, oil-painting feel` |
| Modern money, science, how-things-work, today's world | **Bright** | `modern setting, bright warm sunlight, blue sky, vivid saturated colors, cheerful energy` |
| Crime, scams, mysteries, investigations (any era) | **Night** | `night, moonlight and neon or desk-lamp light, deep blues with gold highlights, cinematic shadows` |

## Cast
| Character | Animal | Plays | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coon** (host) | Raccoon | The one who knows — hook + ending, reacts, explains | Black three-piece suit, gold tie, pocket square, amber eyes, calm knowing smile (see ref) |
| **The Suits** | Owls | Officials, bankers, judges, rule-makers | Grey suits or robes, spectacles, ledgers |
| **Fat Cats** | Large cats (lynx / Persian) | The rich, CEOs, kings | Velvet, gold chains, rings, heavy-lidded eyes |
| **The Crew** | Beavers | Workers, builders, ordinary people on the job | Work clothes, rolled sleeves, tool belts |
| **Townsfolk** | Squirrels / rabbits | Everyday people, the crowd | Era-appropriate everyday clothes |
| **The Law** | Bulldogs / German shepherds | Police, FBI, soldiers, guards | Uniforms of the era |
| **Foxes** | Foxes | Fictional scammers / villains (never a real named person) | Slick tailoring, sly grin |
| **Crows** | Crows | Doubters, gossip, the laughing crowd | Flat caps, pointing |

Real people are never drawn with a recognizable face: hat silhouette, from behind, or in shadow.

## Prompt template (attach ref/coon-main.webp + any approved cast image)
```
Vertical 9:16 rich digital painting, same art style and the same raccoon character as the attached image: mature anthropomorphic animals with adult proportions, realistic animal faces, textured fur, tailored clothes, visible brush texture, cinematic warm-and-cool lighting, detailed background with depth.
```
