# Posting schedule (@coonknows: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)

Goal: **100k TikTok views in a rolling 30 days** (Creator Rewards; we have the 10k followers already).
That's ≈ 3,300 views a day across everything we post. We post as much as we can, inside the rules
in PLAYBOOK §6c.

**Times are US Eastern** until Brian says otherwise. Change the zone here and everything below shifts with it.

## The rules (never break these)
1. **≥ 3 hours between posts.** Two posts close together compete for the same test audience.
2. **A different topic every post.** No reposts, no lightly edited re-uploads, no "part 2".
3. **Every post ≥ 1:02** (stitches too: their clip + our part).
4. **Searchable words in three places:** caption line 1, on-screen text in the first 2 s, first spoken sentence.
5. Same video goes out on all three apps in the same slot (TikTok first, then Reels and Shorts).

## Daily slots (up to 5 posts a day)
| Slot | Time (ET) | What goes here | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 7:00 | *(no post)* Claude runs the trend scrape → `docs/trends/<date>.md` | topics ready before the first post |
| 1 | 9:00 | **Stitch** of a viral video from the trend sheet ("here's what's actually going on") | borrows a big audience at the start of the day |
| 2 | 12:00 | **Timely topic** (news, a holiday, a rumor everyone's searching) | timely won before (the stock-up debunk) |
| 3 | 15:00 | **True story** about a real person or event | biggest hit so far (Victor Romero) |
| 4 | 18:00 | **Money / how-it-works** explainer | evening scroll; money numbers get saves |
| 5 | 21:00 | **Second timely topic or a myth debunk** ("Is it true…", "Did… really…") | late scroll, search-driven |

If we only have 2–3 videos ready, fill slots **1, 2, 3** first, then 5, then 4. Never squeeze two into one 3-hour window.
**Post the day's two strongest videos in the 12:00 and 21:00 slots.**

## Daily routine
| When | Who | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00 | Claude | Trend scrape → topics + search phrases + stitch links |
| 7:30 | Brian | Pick from the sheet; Claude starts scripts |
| through the day | Brian | **≈ 15 min commenting:** one real comment on 3–5 bigger history/explainer creators' new posts within their first hour |
| each slot | Brian | Post with the `post.md` caption; reply to the first comments in the first 30 min (replies keep the video in testing longer) |
| 22:00 | Claude (optional) | Quick check of the day's views; adjust tomorrow's slots |

## Weekly check (Sundays)
- Views in the last 30 days vs the 100k line.
- Which slot and which lane got the most views and shares → give that lane more slots next week.
- Brian screenshots from TikTok Studio for the top 3 and bottom 3: average watch time and % who watched to the end.
