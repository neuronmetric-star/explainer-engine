## The format
"Why the f*ck…?" hook → "give me a minute, I'll show you" → the most useful, simplest, most visual
explanation on the internet → punchline → follow. Any topic: history, science, money, body, space, tech.

## Rules that protect the money
1. **Every video 61–75 s.** Under 60 s earns $0 from Creator Rewards.
2. **One swear, in the hook only.** On-screen caption is masked (F*CK). Never in the post caption,
   hashtags, title or thumbnail. Don't swear about real people, tragedies or kids' topics.
3. **Original content only** — our engine, our models, our voice script. No reposted clips.
4. **Facts with sources** — pin a "sources" comment; it builds trust and protects the account.

## Growth habits (what's working for fast-growing faceless accounts)
- **Post daily.** "Bob figures it out" hit 238K followers with 23 videos in ~4 weeks — volume + one recurring character.
- **Recurring character**: give our presenter a name, a face and a signature outfit (next task).
- **Saves and shares** drive reach: end with "Send this to someone who …"; educational videos get saved more.
- **Series**: number them ("Why the F*ck #12"), so people binge the profile.
- **Same video → TikTok + Shorts + Reels** the same day.
