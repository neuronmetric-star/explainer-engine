# Study — "NVIDIA Explained Like You're 5" (Crayon Capital)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1GowFTjbUnk · 14:30 · ~2,200 spoken words (~150 wpm) · 1.76M views · 27K likes
Channel: 307K subs. Auto-dubbed into 20 languages. Paid sponsor (BetterHelp). Keywords include "Oversimplified style".

## Structure (chapters = stakes, not topics)
| Time | Chapter title | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Two Years Building the Wrong Chip | cold open teaser → childhood → Denny's founding → NV1 flop → Sega bailout |
| 5:43 | The $500M Bet on PhD Students Nobody Wanted | Riva 128 saves them → GeForce → CUDA bet nobody understood |
| 9:18 | The 2012 Competition That Proved Jensen Right | ImageNet/AlexNet → the bet pays off |
| 11:03 | Blocked at Every Turn, $3 Trillion Valuation Anyway | ARM deal blocked → China export bans → $5T anyway → closing quote |

## The formula
1. **Cold open = the whole arc in 25 s**: humble start → absurd size now → "not from the safe bet but a gamble nobody believed in" → villain incoming ("the government moved to bring him down").
2. **Loop**: bet → everyone doubts → near-death → payoff → bigger bet. Repeated 4 times, stakes rise each time.
3. **Dialogue scenes** carry the story — characters talk ("I think it should be us." / "Jensen, I have a mortgage…" / "I hate that this sounds insane and also kind of logical"). Narrator only bridges.
4. **Comedy every ~20 s**: dry one-liners ("We built the wrong chip with tremendous confidence", "That's not a win. That's a murder scene", "buy a very large leather jacket museum").
5. **ELI5 analogies** for every hard idea: CPU = one very smart employee; GPU = 10,000 interns doing tiny sums at once.
6. **Exact numbers** as punchlines: 30 days of cash, $5M, 1M units in 4 months, $500M, 26.2% vs 15.3% error.
7. **Ending = founder's own quote**, no hard CTA.

## Retention (YouTube "most replayed")
- Peaks: 0:00 (hook), 1:18 ("I think it should be us"), **5:13–5:39 = top of the whole video** (people skip the sponsor and land on "The money is coming"), 9:18 (ImageNet reveal), 10:52–11:30 (Washington calls).
- Dips: 4:00–5:10 (sponsor read), 4:40–5:00 lowest.
- Lesson: put the sponsor **inside a cliffhanger** (Jensen waiting on Sega's answer) — viewers sit through it to get the payoff.
