# Babytopia — long-form channel plan (DRAFT, decisions pending with Brian)

## Concept
"Anything, explained like you're 5" — companies, history, books, animals, science. 6–10 min, 16:9 YouTube.
World: **Babytopia** — everyone is a toddler, but fully dressed and premium-rendered (rich painting / cinematic
light, same quality bar as the Coonverse). The joke writes itself: billion-dollar decisions made by babies.
Original look — not stick figures, not any existing film's baby characters.

## Production math (10 min ≈ 1,400–1,500 words)
- Shots change every ~4–6 s → ~110 shots. Do NOT make 110 unique images: reuse **set plates** (dorm, office,
  courtroom…) + **character pose layers** (green screen) + engine motion. Target ~35–50 plates + ~6 characters × 5 poses.
- Engine work: landscape 1920×1080 mode, chapter title cards, dialogue captions (speaker + italic line at top),
  multi-voice alignment (one voice file per speaker line or one combined file), music bed ducking.
- Shorts: every long video gets cut into 3–5 vertical Shorts (@coonknows style) for promotion.

## First episode: Facebook / Meta (outline — every fact to be sourced before scripting)
Cold open: a 19-year-old builds a site in his dorm that billions now check daily; turns down $1B at 22;
gets sued by his own best friend; then bets the company on a world that doesn't exist yet.
1. **The Dorm** — FaceMash (2003) → TheFacebook (Feb 2004); the Winklevoss twins say he stole their idea.
2. **The Billion-Dollar No** — Thiel's angel check; Yahoo's $1B offer (2006) refused; co-founder Saverin diluted.
3. **Buy It or Bury It** — ads machine; 2012 IPO; Instagram $1B (2012), WhatsApp $19B (2014).
4. **The Scandal** — Cambridge Analytica (2018) → $5B FTC penalty (2019).
5. **The Bet on a World That Doesn't Exist** — rename to Meta (2021), metaverse losses, stock crash, comeback.
Close on his own motto ("Move fast and break things") as the punchline.
ELI5 analogies: social graph = a yearbook that updates itself; "if it's free, you're the product" = the
toy is free because the toy shop sells your attention; acquisitions = buying the kid who might steal your lunch table.
