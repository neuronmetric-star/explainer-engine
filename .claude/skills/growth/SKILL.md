# Growth lane: endings, CTAs, shares, cadence, search

Scope: educational and ad-safe only. Never invent numbers; if something wasn't reported, write "not reported".
Evidence key: `[@handle, plays]`. **TEST** = weak evidence. Rates marked "(computed)" are my arithmetic on the reported stats.
Big winners (1M+): @thebuildingbox 20.3M, @unnecessaryengineering 14.8M, @justrichit 4.9M.

Web-research tags: OFFICIAL = the platform itself; VENDOR = tool/agency blog, usually no stated method (the Emplifi numbers come from Facebook Reels brand accounts, sample size not reported, text and tables partly disagree); CREATOR = a coach's teaching. ## Rules

### Endings
1. **The ending is the share trigger.** Pick one: irony flip, wide reveal, or one visual metaphor.
   - [@unnecessaryengineering, 14.8M, 163.9K shares: irony punchline; the study says it makes people tag friends]; [@thebuildingbox, 20.3M, 34.6K shares: pull-back reveal + "Congratulations!"]; [@justrichit, 4.9M, 14.2K shares: balance-scale visual].
   - The "makes people tag friends" reason is the study's reading, not measured.
2. **No spoken "follow" ask.** [@thebuildingbox, @unnecessaryengineering, @justrichit: all three 1M+ videos had no spoken CTA]. **TEST:** this is a correlation across n = 3, not proof a CTA hurts. Compare against a CTA version in the lessons table.
3. **If a CTA is needed, put it on a prop inside the scene** [@cardboard_cinema, 110.3K: a poop bag reading "LIKE & FOLLOW"]. Sub-1M, so **TEST**.
4. **Keep the signature ending.** The helicopter + city-sunset ending [house: CLAUDE.md] is a wide reveal, so it fits rule 1 (my inference, not studied).
5. **Give a useful fact as the share reason.** [@justrichit, 4.9M: bumped passengers can be owed cash, up to $2,000 per the video]. Verify any such figure before reuse [house: sources.md].
6. **TEST: validation ending** ("You were never X, your brain just…") [@sketchedpsychology, 246.3K: 3.5K shares ≈ 1.4% of plays (computed)]. Sub-1M.
7. **TEST: moral ending** [@nolledge, 85.4K: 439 shares ≈ 0.5% of plays (computed)]. This evidence is weaker than the general-lane note claims.

### Comments
8. **A spoken comment question boosts comments, not necessarily shares.** [@sketchedpsychology, 246.3K: the only video that asked aloud ("Does a messy room affect your mood?"); 168 comments, the most in the General batch]. n = 1, so **TEST**.
9. **A question only in the caption doesn't pull replies.** [@mr.benny.tv, 17.5K: caption question, 5 comments].
10. **Leave a deliberate, answerable gap.** [@thebuildingbox, 20.3M, 5.6K comments: comments came from gaps the video left open ("what about rain?", "sulfur gas?")]. The study's reading, so **TEST**. Never leave a factual error as bait.
11. **Keep the one-word viewer question** where the trend-ride mode asks for it [house: CLAUDE.md pipeline step 1].

### Saves and loops
12. **Cheat-sheet value drives saves.** [@finance_with_stickman, 37.8K: 805 saves ≈ 2.1% of plays (computed)]. Sub-1M. Use the do-it-yourself last line [house: PLAYBOOK §3.5].
13. **TEST: seamless loop** [@smart.doctor, 620.7K, 20 s]. It's a short-clip tactic; our 65 s videos must pass 61 s, so use only a callback, not a loop [@mr.benny.tv, 17.5K].

### Search and caption
14. **Caption line 1 = the search phrase; 3–5 niche hashtags, never only #fyp, no swearing.** [house: PLAYBOOK §6b]; [@sketchedpsychology, 246.3K and @unnecessaryengineering, 14.8M hooks target searchable phrases].
15. **Long explanations go in the caption, not on screen.** [@smart.doctor, 620.7K: long text in the post caption]. **TEST.**

### Cadence
16. **≥ 3 h apart, a different topic every post, every post ≥ 1:02.** [house: `docs/posting-schedule.md`, CLAUDE.md]. The studies didn't report cadence data: not reported.
17. **Weekly check (Sundays):** log views, 3-second hold, watch-to-end, shares, saves in the lessons table. The best and worst video each produce one rule [house: CLAUDE.md].

### Template sameness
18. **Avoid mass-produced, repetitive videos.** [platform rule: since July 15, 2025 YouTube withholds monetization from "inauthentic" (mass-produced or repetitive) content; TechCrunch, Social Media Today]. [OFFICIAL, Instagram: pushing to favor originals].
   - Keep the brand frame (cast, narrator, ending) but vary the topic, hook form, ladder shape and joke on each video.
   - Rotate CTAs and hook forms; don't post two videos in a row with the same skeleton and same lines.
   - Narration and art are original every time; never reuse another creator's script or art.
   - The studies don't define how repetitive is "too" repetitive: not reported.

### Added from the web research [source: How educator creators go viral.md]
19. **What each platform rewards.** TikTok Creator Rewards scores originality, play duration, search value and engagement, and pays only for original videos over 1 minute [OFFICIAL, TikTok Newsroom]. Instagram's top signals are watch time, likes and sends, with sends weighing more for reaching non-followers [OFFICIAL, Social Media Today, Jan 2025]. YouTube monetization uses engaged views; judge it by engaged views and viewed-vs-swiped-away, not raw views [OFFICIAL, TechCrunch]. Not published anywhere: any retention threshold for wider distribution, and TikTok's For You weights: not reported.
20. **End on the payoff and cut; no outro card.** [web research, Growth lane]. Our signature city-sunset shot is a picture, not a card, but check it doesn't read as one.
21. **TEST: a share-framed line** ("send this to…") instead of "follow for more". Sends carry weight on Instagram. No data on how much follow or save prompts lift results: not reported. This is a spoken ask, so Brian decides.
22. **Write the dry last line so people want to send it,** or loop it into the first line [web research, contradictions table].
23. **Seamless loops help very short clips only:** +18.7% replays and +23.6% reach for videos of 7 s or less [VENDOR, Emplifi]. Not applicable to our 65 s+ videos (refines rule 13).
24. **TEST: audience-request loops** ("comment what I should do next", "you asked for it") recur in the leads. Those titles came from a banned API; recheck by watching.
25. **Run named, numbered series with the same character** [web research; ours: `THE HECK?` banner, `docs/series.md`].
26. **Cadence: no platform states an ideal frequency (not reported).** One faceless-channel case took about 60 videos and about 6 months to reach 1,000 subscribers [Kapwing]. Keep the house rules in rule 16.
27. **Post natively to TikTok, Shorts and Reels with clean exports (no watermarks).** Test hooks with Instagram Trial Reels (shown to non-followers only; Reels up to 3 minutes can now be recommended).
28. **Pick topics from search:** TikTok search suggestions, the Creative Center's Keyword Insights, YouTube autocomplete. Say the search phrase out loud and show it on screen in the opening [web research, rule 5].
29. **Narrator voice risk, TEST/unconfirmed:** the research says YouTube's later revision reportedly flags "AI personas" (date unconfirmed), and advises a human or clearly original voice. Our narrator is a synthesized ElevenLabs voice [CLAUDE.md pipeline step 5]. Flag it to Brian; no action taken.

## Pre-publish checklist
- [ ] Does the last 5 s end on an irony flip, a wide reveal or one visual metaphor?
- [ ] Is the CTA decision (none, prop CTA, or the CLAUDE.md follow line) made on purpose and logged?
- [ ] Is there a viewer question where we want comments, spoken, not only in the caption?
- [ ] Does the share reason (flip, useful fact or validation) hold up when someone tags a friend?
- [ ] Is caption line 1 the search phrase, with 3–5 niche hashtags and no swearing?
- [ ] Is it ≥ 1:02 and ≥ 3 h after the last post, on a different topic?
- [ ] Does it differ from the last 3 posts in hook form, ladder shape and ending?
- [ ] Is the post's numbers row ready for the Sunday lessons table?
